1. Sign in

Where staff sign in with their own email and password on a new device, after signing out, or every 7 days.

What it does

  • Signs one person into the dashboard with their own email and password. Every person has a personal login; there is no shared staff login (decided v0.13), so the change log records real names.
  • The full password is needed on a new device, after signing out, and every 7 days. In between, a locked screen reopens with the person's 4-digit PIN instead (see the lock screen).
  • There is no "Create account" link. Admins add staff from Staff accounts in the account menu.
  • No rail or top bar on this screen. Nothing is shown until someone is signed in.

How staff use it

  • Type email, type password, click or tap Sign in. They land on Home.
  • If the email or password is wrong, an amber message appears above the button and tells them what to do (phone view below shows this state).
  • Proposed (needs your OK): no self-service "reset password" link. Staff ask an admin, who resets it from Staff accounts.

Rules applied

  • 5 — personal login (email + password) for every person (v0.13); password required on a new device, after sign-out, and every 7 days (v0.14).
  • 5 / 6 — Admin and Staff levels; only admins add staff and reset passwords (v0.15).
  • 1.1 — one primary button only ("Sign in"). 7 — primary button is school blue; school red appears only inside the logo.
  • 1.4 / 7 — error says what happened and what to do, in amber with ti-alert-triangle, never red.
  • 2.1 — no full-width buttons on PC or tablet; the phone uses the one allowed full-width submit button.
  • 8 — labels above fields; 2.1 / 2.5 — fields 32px (36px on phones), buttons 32–36px tall; touch devices get 44px tap areas through invisible padding.

2. Home (empty, day one)

The first screen after sign-in: a welcome header and one line saying Home fills in as sections are added.

What it does

  • Header shows the school logo, a time-of-day greeting ("Good evening"), today's date, and the active location. The header stays permanently, even after Home has content.
  • Under the header, one gray line: "Nothing on Home yet. Items appear here as sections are added." It disappears as soon as any section adds an item to Home. There is no button, because there is nothing to add yet.
  • The rail (PC, tablet) and bottom bar (phone) show only Home, because Home is the only section that exists. No placeholder or "coming soon" icons.
  • The top bar shows only controls that work today: the location switcher and the account avatar (initials). Search, notifications, and add new appear only when a section needs them.

How staff use it

  • They land here after signing in. Unlocking returns them to wherever they were.
  • The location switcher (Worcester | Shrewsbury | Both) sets which school Home shows. Today it only changes the location text in the header; once sections feed Home, it filters what they show. Each device opens on its own default (the Shrewsbury tablet opens on Shrewsbury).
  • Click or tap the initials circle to open the account menu (lock, PIN, password, sign out).

Rules applied

  • P7 — shell shows only sections that exist: Home is the only rail / bottom-bar item.
  • 9 — Home header: logo, greeting, date, active location (v0.11); empty line removed once real items exist.
  • 8 — empty state with no action available shows the line alone, no dummy button.
  • 6 — top bar shows only working controls; day one is location switcher + account menu (v0.12).
  • 3.3 — location switcher on every screen; default stored per device (tablet shows Shrewsbury).
  • 1.3 / 2.3 — PC rail is icon-only (label on hover); tablet rail and phone bottom bar show labels.
  • 2.5 — touch frames use 44px tap areas for the switcher, avatar, and nav items.

3. Account menu (open)

Opens from the initials circle, top-right. Personal account actions for everyone, plus an Admin group for admins.

What it does

  • Top line shows who is signed in: name, email and account level (Admin or Staff), so nobody works under the wrong person by mistake.
  • Lock now locks the screen immediately. Use it when you step away from the desk or the mat. If you forget, the device locks itself after 45 minutes idle.
  • Change PIN sets your own 4-digit unlock PIN (the one used on the lock screen).
  • Change password changes the password you sign in with (email + password).
  • This device: Worcester sets which location this device opens on. It is stored per device, not per person, so the Shrewsbury tablet opens on Shrewsbury no matter who signs in. The tablet below shows "Shrewsbury" for that reason.
  • Admin group (admins only): Staff accounts to add staff, disable an account, or reset someone's password or PIN; Settings for school-wide settings, including turning keyboard shortcuts on or off.
  • Sign out ends your session on this device. It is shown in normal text, not red, because red is reserved for delete/remove.
  • Staff-level people see the same menu without the "Admin" label and its two items.

How staff use it

  • PC and tablet: click or tap your initials (JB). The menu drops down under it; click an item, or click anywhere else (or press Esc on the PC) to close it.
  • Phone: tap your initials and the menu slides up from the bottom as a sheet, within thumb reach. Tap the dimmed area or swipe the sheet down to close it.
  • Most common use: Lock now before walking away, then the PIN to get back in.

Rules applied

  • 6 — account menu contents and order (v0.15); admin items under an "Admin" label.
  • 5 — two account levels, Admin and Staff; 45-minute auto-lock; personal 4-digit PIN.
  • 3.3 — default location is stored per device, not per person.
  • 3.6 — keyboard shortcuts can be turned off in Settings.
  • 7 — red only for delete/remove, so Sign out is not red.
  • 2.4 — icons from the master map only (ti-lock, ti-key, ti-password, ti-map-pin, ti-user-cog, ti-settings, ti-logout).
  • 2.5 — 44px menu rows on touch; 32px rows with a mouse.

4. Lock screen

Appears after 45 minutes idle or when someone picks "Lock now". Hides the screen until the signed-in person enters their PIN.

What it does

  • The screen stays on the person who was signed in ("Locked", Jeffrey) and hides everything behind it, so parents at the mat or the desk can't see records while staff are away.
  • The 4-digit PIN is not a login. It only reopens your own already-signed-in screen. It can't be used to sign in as anyone or on another device.
  • Nothing unsaved is lost when it locks: the session stays alive in the background, and you land back exactly where you were.
  • 5 wrong PINs in a row turn the PIN off for that session, and the full password is needed instead. The phone shows the warning after a wrong PIN: how many tries are left.
  • The full email + password is also asked for every 7 days, after signing out, and on a new device.
  • Not you? Sign out signs Jeffrey out so the next person can sign in with their own email and password.

How staff use it

  • PC: just type the 4 digits on the keyboard, no clicking needed. The 4th digit unlocks, so there is no button. The PC shows 2 of 4 digits typed.
  • Tablet and phone: tap the digits on the on-screen keypad; "Del" removes the last digit. The 4th digit unlocks.
  • Wrong PIN: the dots clear and an amber message says how many tries are left (shown on the phone).
  • Someone else needs the device: tap "Not you? Sign out", then they sign in normally.

Rules applied

  • 5 — 45-minute auto-lock; personal 4-digit PIN; 5 wrong PINs → password; password every 7 days; "Not you? Sign out".
  • 7 — errors are amber with ti-alert-triangle, never red; school blue for PIN dots, avatar and link only.
  • 1.4 — error says what happened and what to do next.
  • 2.4 — ti-lock marks the locked state. The delete-digit key is the word "Del", because ti-arrow-left already means Back.
  • 2.1 / 2.5 — keypad keys are drawn 36px tall; the 8px gaps between keys give each one a full 44px tap area without overlap.
  • 1.1 — no primary button; the 4th digit completes the action.
