Forms — mockups for approval

Jae H. Jeong's U.S. Taekwondo Center · New students, step 3: forms · guidelines v0.85 · October 17, 2026 · revision 1

Design only — not a working app. Nothing here is built or deployed. Dashboard screens are shown at PC (1024×800), tablet held upright (768×1024) and phone (375px); public pages are mostly shown on a phone, since that's where families fill them in. Names, times and wording are made-up samples. "Today" is pretended to be Monday, January 11, 2027.

Decided in chat (written into the rulebook once you approve)

  1. Builder: three panels on PC — field types on the left, the form in the middle, settings for the chosen field on the right. Drag fields in; any field type, reorder, sections, "show only if" rules.
  2. Public forms come in short steps with a progress bar; each section in the builder is one step.
  3. Families get a thank-you screen (wording set per form), a confirmation email and an "Add to calendar" button.
  4. Staff get a count badge in the dashboard (shown with step 4, Prospects) and an email to the school for each new submission.
  5. Siblings: "+ Add another child" on any form; each child can book their own free class.
  6. Waiver block can be added to any form: text, "I agree" checkbox, finger or mouse signature.
  7. Booking: the form offers only the classes and times the admin picks from the Schedule, each time with a spot limit; full, closed and cancelled times disappear. Each form sets its own booking window (how many days ahead, how much notice).
  8. Sharing: a link, a QR code, and kiosk mode for the front-desk tablet (start screen, clears itself after submit or 2 minutes idle, staff PIN to leave). Staff can also fill in a form for a family.
  9. Spam: Cloudflare Turnstile (usually invisible). Privacy: prospects who never join are deleted after 2 years; all staff can see phone and email.
  10. Admins build and edit forms; staff can view forms, share them and fill them in for a family.

Still needs your OK — details I added

  1. Rail icon ti-clipboard-text, label "Forms", after Calendar.
  2. A form is Draft (not public yet), Live (green tag), or Closed (link shows "This form is closed").
  3. "+ New form" starts from a template: Free class (social media), Walk-in, Event, or Blank. Templates are just starting points; everything can be changed.
  4. Every form always asks for parent name, phone and email and has a Children block (first name, age). These can be relabeled but not removed — a prospect needs a way to be called back.
  5. The builder saves as you go, but families only see changes after you press Publish.
  6. When nothing is selected, the right panel shows Form settings: thank-you message, confirmation email on/off, which school, and Live/Closed.
  7. Booking times are listed per class from the Schedule, each with its own spot limit (default 3). If the Schedule changes, new times appear with the default limit.
  8. Public link looks like tkd-dashboard.pages.dev/f/free-class (later your school's own web address). You can edit the last part.
  9. On the public form a full or closed time is hidden, not shown grayed. If no times are left, the family sees "No free-class times are open right now. Leave your details and we'll call you." and can still submit.
  10. The school email alert goes to one address you set in the form settings.
  11. Field-type icons added to the icon map: ti-cursor-text, ti-align-left, ti-phone, ti-mail, ti-123, ti-circle-dot, ti-checkbox, ti-selector, ti-heading, ti-separator-horizontal, ti-users, ti-signature, plus ti-share, ti-qrcode, ti-device-tablet, ti-eye, ti-copy, ti-lock, ti-git-branch.
  12. A family who books gets their spot right away (no approval step); staff can still call to confirm.
