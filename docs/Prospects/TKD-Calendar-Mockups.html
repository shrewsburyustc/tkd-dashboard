Calendar — mockups for approval

Jae H. Jeong's U.S. Taekwondo Center · New students, step 2: calendar · guidelines v0.233 · October 17, 2026 · revision 1

Design only — not a working app. Nothing here is built or deployed. Screens are shown at PC (1024×800), tablet held upright (768×1024), and phone (375px). Dates, events and names are made-up samples; "today" is pretended to be Monday, January 11, 2027. Classes come from the approved Schedule (the Winter schedule starts Jan 4).

Decided in chat (written into the rulebook once you approve)

  1. The Calendar shows regular classes (from Schedule), closures, school events and free-class bookings (bookings appear once step 3, Forms, is built).
  2. Closures can close a whole day or only some classes. Cancelled classes stay visible, crossed out.
  3. Events can run several days, have start and end times (or be all day), belong to one school or both, and have notes.
  4. Event colors come from event types — admins set up types (Testing, Tournament…) and pick each type's color.
  5. Opens in Month view; each day box shows a summary (events, closures and bookings written out; classes as a count). Week view is one click away.
  6. Clicking a day opens it in a side panel on PC. Phone: small month with dots, chosen day listed underneath.
  7. Admins add and edit; staff view only.

Still needs your OK — details I added

  1. Rail icon ti-calendar-month, label "Calendar", after Schedule. (ti-calendar is already used for date fields.)
  2. One blue "+ Add" button opens a small menu: Event or Closure.
  3. Closures are amber with an icon (they are a warning: classes are cancelled). In Week view the closed hours are striped amber.
  4. Free-class bookings show as a white outlined chip with ti-user-plus ("2 free classes"); in the side panel each one lists the name, time and class.
  5. A dashed chip marks the day a planned schedule starts ("Winter schedule starts").
  6. The side panel shows today until you click another day; clicking an event or closure there opens it for editing.
  7. Tablet held upright: month on top, chosen day underneath (a side panel would squeeze the month too much).
  8. Month view shows the weeks of that month only; days from the next/previous month are grayed.
  9. Removing an event or closure is instant with Undo. Deleting an event type that's in use asks to confirm.
  10. Event types are managed from ⋯ → "Event types", using the same 8 colors as classes (no red).
  11. Week view has an "All day" row on top for all-day and multi-day events.
  12. On Both, each chip gets a small school letter (W or S), and class counts add up both schools.
