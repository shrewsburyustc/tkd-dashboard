Prospects — mockups for approval

Jae H. Jeong's U.S. Taekwondo Center · New students, step 4: prospects list · guidelines v0.62 · October 17, 2026 · revision 2 (your 13 details applied)

Design only — not a working app. Nothing here is built or deployed. Screens are shown at PC (1024×800), tablet held upright (768×1024) and phone (375px). All names and numbers are made-up samples. "Today" is pretended to be Monday, January 11, 2027.

Decided in chat (written into the rulebook once you approve)

  1. Pipeline bar + list: stage counts across the top (New, Contacted, Booked, Came, Joined); click a stage to show only those people, click again for everyone.
  2. One row per child. Siblings share the family's contact details and are linked to each other.
  3. Statuses: start with New, Contacted, Booked, Came, Joined, No-show, Not interested; admins can edit the list.
  4. Follow-up tools: notes and call log, follow-up reminders ("Follow up today"), tap to call / text / email, and marking Came / No-show (also from the Calendar).
  5. Joined prospects have their own place (the Joined stage) until a Students section exists.
  6. Clicking a prospect opens a side panel next to the list (phone: full screen).

Details — reviewed one by one and approved (changes from revision 1 are marked changed)

  1. Rail icon ti-address-book, label "Prospects", after Forms, with a dark count badge = new sign-ups nobody has opened yet.
  2. Prospects opens on "Follow up today" (amber chip): everyone whose follow-up date is today or overdue, plus brand-new sign-ups. Overdue dates show amber.
  3. No-show and Not interested are chips beside the bar, not stages (they're side exits). Joined is the last stage, with a green bar.
  4. Status rules the app keeps, even when admins edit the list: a sign-up with a booking starts as Booked, without one as New; marking the free class sets Came or No-show. These built-in statuses can be renamed and recolored but not deleted; admins can add their own (e.g. "Trial 2") and reorder them.
  5. changed Staff mark attendance — never automatic. On the free-class day a Check in button shows (in the prospect's panel and the Calendar's day panel); tap it when the family arrives → Came. If nobody checked them in by the time class ends, a reminder asks "Did Ava come?" Came / No-show until someone answers. No-show sets a call-back for tomorrow.
  6. Log a call: type a note, then pick when to follow up (Tomorrow, In 3 days, Next week, Pick a date, No follow-up). Each note keeps who wrote it and when.
  7. Call / Text / Email open the phone's dialer, texting app or email app. On a PC, Call shows the number large to dial. Nothing is sent by the dashboard.
  8. changed "+ Add prospect" asks which form to use (e.g. Walk-in) and opens it for staff to fill in for the family — the same questions as the real form.
  9. The panel also shows the form they came from, all their answers, and the signed waiver (if any).
  10. changed Staff can do everything here, including editing the status list. Only deleting a prospect is admins only (with a confirmation).
  11. changed Columns: Child · Parent · Free class · Follow up · Status. Each school's prospects stay separate: on Both, Worcester's list and Shrewsbury's list show one after the other, each with its own pipeline bar — never mixed (screen 9).
  12. Phone bottom bar: with six sections there isn't room for all of them, so it shows Home, Calendar, Prospects, More; More (ti-menu-2, already in the rulebook) lists Testing, Schedule and Forms.
  13. Icons: ti-address-book, ti-message (text), ti-notes, ti-user-check (Came), ti-user-x (No-show), ti-clock-hour-4 (follow-up date).
