  • One place to keep complete: a dated thing added later goes into this reader.

Recommendation: B. You want everything, now and later — one reader is the easiest place to keep it complete and private, and it doesn't touch the other sessions' files. A is quicker today but spreads the work across eight reads and three other sessions' fake-data files.
