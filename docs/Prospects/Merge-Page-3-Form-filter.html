Page 3 — the form filter, open and applied

Drawn on the real page (fake data; the form names and counts in the drawing are examples). Amber outline = new or changed. Open: tick one form, several, or "All forms". Each form shows how many prospects it has; with Both schools the forms are grouped under Worcester and Shrewsbury. On a phone it opens as a bottom sheet with big rows. Applied: the button turns blue, the line under it says what is filtered with Show all, and the stage numbers (and the Board) count only the picked forms. It resets on reload, sign-in or lock.
