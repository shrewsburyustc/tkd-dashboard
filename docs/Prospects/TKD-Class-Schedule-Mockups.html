Schedule — mockups for approval

Jae H. Jeong's U.S. Taekwondo Center · New students, step 1: class schedule · guidelines v0.59 · October 17, 2026 · revision 2 (calendar look and class colors)

Design only — not a working app. Nothing here is built or deployed. Each screen is shown at PC (1024×800), tablet held upright (768×1024), and phone (375px). Class times are made-up samples — the real ones get entered later. Dashed amber notes are review callouts, not part of the design.

Decided in chat (to be written into the rulebook once you approve)

  1. Schedule is its own rail section (not inside Calendar).
  2. PC and tablet show a week grid; the phone shows a list by day.
  3. A class on the schedule is a group from Testing → Classes and ranks (White belt, Group A, Group B…). No second list.
  4. The schedule repeats every week until it's changed. You can plan as many future schedules as you like, each with its own start date.
  5. Admins edit, staff view only.
  6. Calendar look: times down the left side, and each box is as tall as the class is long. Every hour from the first class to the last is shown.
  7. Phone shows one day at a time in the same look, with Mon–Sun buttons to switch days.
  8. Classes are color-coded — you pick each class's color.

Still needs your OK — details I added

  1. Rail icon ti-clock, label "Schedule" (Calendar will get ti-calendar later).
  2. One schedule is shown at a time. A dropdown at the top picks Current or a planned one ("Winter · starts Jan 5, 2027").
  3. Editing the current schedule takes effect right away. For a change that starts later, use "Plan a change".
  4. A new planned schedule starts as a copy of whichever schedule runs just before its start date (or blank, if you pick that).
  5. Adding a time slot can pick several days at once (e.g. Mon and Wed at 4:30) — it makes one slot per day.
  6. Removing a time slot is instant with Undo (5-second toast). Deleting a whole planned schedule asks to confirm first.
  7. All 7 days always show; a day with no classes is an empty column. Dotted lines mark the half hours.
  8. Color is picked in the time slot dialog from 8 soft colors (gray, yellow, orange, green, teal, sky, purple, pink) and applies to every slot of that class. Red is not offered (red only means delete). New classes start gray.
  9. Each box shows the class name and its time. A very short class (under about 40 minutes) shows the name only.
  10. When there are more hours than fit, the schedule scrolls; it opens at the first class of the day.
  11. On Both, the two schools are shown one above the other (view and edit both work, each slot belongs to its school).
  12. No free-class spot limits here — those are set on each booking form in step 3, as decided.
