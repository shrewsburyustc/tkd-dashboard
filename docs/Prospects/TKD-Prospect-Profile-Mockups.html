New students: date of birth, address, profile and printout

Mockups for Jeffrey's addition of Nov 4, 2026 (rev 2, after the design review). Nothing is built yet. Amber outlines mark what is new or changed. Each screen fills the window: make the window narrower to see the tablet (640–1023 px) and phone (under 640 px) looks.

Screens

  • 1. The family form, step 1 — each child's last name (v0.637) and date of birth (the dashboard's calendar) instead of the age, and the home address (the new "Address" question: street, city, state, ZIP; decided v0.634, option A).
  • 2. The family form, last step — the signature with its date signed, filled in automatically.
  • 3a. The prospect profile — clicking a child opens a big window (the whole page on a phone): the form they filled out at the top, then everything at a glance.
  • 3b. The profile with "Hide private details" on — date of birth, email, home address and the answers read "Hidden on this tablet"; Email and Print gray, each with one line.
  • 3c. Another state — entered at the desk, an older sign-up (typed age, no address), and "Did Leo come?" under the progress track.
  • 4. The printed visitor sheet — one letter-size page per child: the form at the top, every answer, the waiver with the signature and date signed, and 15 trial-class boxes at the bottom.

What stays the same

  • Parent name, phone and email, the free-class booking step, the Board and List, statuses, follow-ups, notes and history work as today.
  • The staff Fill in dialog and the front-desk kiosk show the same form as the family page, so they get the same new fields.
  • Ages still show in the dashboard (never in the list, v0.245); they are worked out from the date of birth. Children who signed up before this change keep their typed age.
  • The signature picture still opens with Show signature and every look is recorded (v0.242); printing counts as a look. It is still deleted 90 days after the free class; the date signed stays.
Privacy (rule 5): the printout shows a minor's date of birth and home address, and the signature. On screen, "Hide private details" hides them and Print is off. Paper can't be hidden or deleted after 2 years (or the signature after 90 days) like the dashboard's records, so the sheets need a locked drawer and shredding when they're done with.
