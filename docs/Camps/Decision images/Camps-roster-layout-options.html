Camp roster — less on screen, faster decisions

Same camp, same moment (April Vacation Camp, Wed, Apr 21, 9:10 AM). Nothing is lost in either option — prices, extras, paying method and phones stay one tap away. The difference is how the roster is split.

A. Three tabs, one job each: Check in · Payments · Attendance Recommended

The camp page gets three tabs. Check in is the door list: only today's kids, big name, one button, a small amber note only when something needs attention (drop-off, pick-up, not paid). Payments is who owes what, with Mark paid. Attendance is the week grid. During camp week the page opens on Check in; before camp it opens on Payments. Tap a name anywhere → Camper detail (everything else).

Works the same on the mat-side tablet.

Pros

  • At the door you see only names and one button; decide in a glance.
  • Each tab answers one question: who's here? who owes? who came this week?
  • Busy morning and quiet bookkeeping don't fight for space.

Cons

  • Collecting money from a parent at drop-off means switching to Payments (or tapping the "Not paid" note, which opens Mark paid).
  • Three tabs to build instead of one list.

B. One slim list; tap a child to see the rest

Keep one roster, but each row shows only name · one status · one button. Tapping the row opens a small panel under it with days, extras, total, paying method and Mark paid. The week grid stays as the "All days" view.
