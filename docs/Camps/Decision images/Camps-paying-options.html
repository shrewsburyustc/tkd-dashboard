Camps — question 5: how is paying recorded?

Example: April Vacation Camp roster. Payment happens at the desk (cash, check or card terminal) in both options. Amber = where the options differ.

A. One click: Mark paid records the full total Recommended

Same as Color Belt (your call there: "When we click pay, it's paid"). Mark paid records the whole amount owed, with how the family paid; 5-second Undo. If a day or extra is added after paying, the row shows Owes $X and Mark paid records just the difference.

No amounts to type at the desk. Payment method is what the family chose on the form (changeable).

Pros

  • Fastest at the desk: one click, no typing.
  • Works the same as Color Belt — staff learn it once; code is reused.
  • Added days/extras later are still handled (Owes $X).

Cons

  • No deposits or "pay half now" — a family pays the whole amount owed at once.
  • A discount or a different amount needs an admin's Edit payment.

B. Record payment: type any amount (part payments allowed)

Each payment is entered in a small dialog: amount + method. The roster shows what's still due. Allows deposits, paying week by week, or a family paying for siblings in pieces.

A dialog and typing for every payment.

Pros

  • Handles deposits and pay-in-parts.
  • Any amount can be recorded (discounts, odd cases) without an admin.

Cons

  • Slower at the desk: a dialog and typing each time; typing mistakes possible.
  • Different from Color Belt — two payment habits for staff, less code reused.
  • More states to show and test (Paid / $ due / overpaid).
