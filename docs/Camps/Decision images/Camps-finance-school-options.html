Camp payments in the Daily report — which school's drawer?

Example: April Vacation Camp is for both schools, held at Worcester. Mia Rossi's family lives near Shrewsbury and pays $166 cash at the Shrewsbury desk. Single-school camps are simple (their school). This choice only matters for both-schools camps. Amber = what differs.

A. The school whose desk took the money Recommended

Mark paid records the school the device is set to (the Worcester | Shrewsbury switcher). If the switcher is on Both for a both-schools camp, Mark paid asks once: "Paid at which school?" The $166 lands in Shrewsbury's Daily report, where the cash actually is.

On Worcester or Shrewsbury there's no question — one click, as now.

Pros

  • Each school's report matches its own drawer — Close the day always balances.
  • No extra click in the normal case (switcher on a school).

Cons

  • One small extra question when marking paid from the "Both" view.
  • A new field on camp payments (the school) — small database change.
  • A device left on the wrong school records the wrong school (same as any setting; fix with Edit payment).

B. Always the school the camp is held at

No question, no new field. Every payment for April Vacation Camp goes to Worcester's Daily report, wherever it was paid.

Pros

  • Simplest: nothing new on the Camps side.
  • Fine if families always pay where the camp is held.

Cons

  • When a family pays at the other school, both drawers are off (one over, one short) and staff must explain it in the close note.
