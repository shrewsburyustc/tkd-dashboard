Add camp — how to organize it so it isn't everything at once

Same fields as the approved C3, same example: April Vacation Camp from the "Vacation week" template, now for Both schools (held at Worcester). Left: PC dialog. Right: phone. Amber = what changes.

A. Folded sections with a one-line summary Recommended

One page, five sections. Only Camp (name, school, days) is open at the start; the others are folded and show a one-line summary of what's inside. From a template those sections are already filled, so most camps are: type the name, tap the days, Save. Click a section to open it and change something. A section with a problem opens itself and shows amber.

Pros

  • Shortest for the common case (from a template): nothing to scroll past.
  • Summaries let you check prices and dates at a glance before saving.
  • Edit camp works the same: open just the section you need.
  • Save works from anywhere — no Next / Back.

Cons

  • A blank camp still needs opening each section (5 clicks).
  • Something wrong inside a folded section is only a summary line until opened (it turns amber and opens on Save).

B. Steps: one part per screen, Next / Back

The same five parts, shown one at a time like the family form: Camp → Times → Prices → Extras → Family form, with a progress line on top. Save is on the last step.

From a template you still tap Next four times to reach Save.

Pros

  • Each screen is small and calm; a new staff member is guided in order.
  • Familiar: same idea as the family form's steps.

Cons

  • From a template: 4 extra taps (Next × 4) to reach Save, through screens that are already filled.
  • Edit camp to change one price still walks through the steps (or needs a jump-to-step bar).
  • Can't see the whole camp at once before saving.
