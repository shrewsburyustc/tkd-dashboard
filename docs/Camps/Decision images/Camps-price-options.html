A. Price list — one line per thing you charge for Recommended

Every price is a line: name, early price, regular price, and whether it's charged once or per day. Full week, day bundles, non-member fee, pizza day, drop-off, pick-up — all just lines. A new service later = Add price line.

Staff tick the lines that apply. "Per day" lines count the days ticked (changeable). Early or regular price is picked by the signup date.

Pros

  • Handles every form you sent: flat price (Columbus), bundles (Thanksgiving), full week + single days (April, summer).
  • Adding a service later (T-shirt, lunch, a field trip) is one new line — no code change.
  • Easy to read and change; one table per camp.

Cons

  • Staff choose "2 days" themselves — the app doesn't pick the bundle for them (it can warn if days ticked ≠ bundle).
  • Non-member is a line staff tick, not automatic from the member box (it can be pre-ticked when "Non-member" is chosen).

B. Price grid by number of days + extras list

Like the old tkd-camps app: a grid with one row per number of days and four price columns (member / non-member × early / regular), plus a separate list of extras. The app picks the price from the days ticked.

Fewer clicks per camper: the base price fills itself in from the days and the member box.

Pros

  • The base price is automatic — staff can't pick the wrong bundle.
  • Member / non-member handled by the grid.

Cons

  • Rigid: anything that isn't "price by number of days" (a field-trip fee, a T-shirt, a sibling discount) needs new columns or code.
  • Many boxes to fill and check, even for a 1-day camp; bigger for summer.
  • Two places for prices (grid + extras) instead of one; more rules hidden in the app ("pizza not for full week").
