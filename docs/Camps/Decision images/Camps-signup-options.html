Camps — question 4: how do campers get onto the roster?

Fields come from your paper forms: child name, age, member or not, parent name / phone / relationship, emergency contact, waiver signature. Example: Columbus Day Camp. Amber = where the options differ.

A. Staff type them in from the paper form (online signup later) Recommended

Families fill out the paper form and pay at the desk, as today. Staff open the camp, press Add camper and copy the form in. The waiver stays on paper; staff tick "Waiver signed on paper". An online form can be added as a later step.

About 1 minute of typing per child. Nothing public — only signed-in staff touch camp data.

Pros

  • Smallest build: staff screens only. No public page, no new security surface, no new Cloudflare setup.
  • Ready soonest — usable for Thanksgiving / winter break camps.
  • Families' process doesn't change.
  • Online signup can be added later on top, reusing the Color Belt family form.

Cons

  • Staff type every form (~1 min each; ~30 min for a big week).
  • Paper waivers still need filing.
  • Typos possible (phone numbers).

B. Families sign up online now (staff can still add from paper)

Like the Color Belt family form: the camp page has a Registration link + QR code. Parents fill the form on their phone — children, days, extras, contacts — sign the waiver on screen, and see the total. They land on the roster as "Not paid"; staff press Mark paid at the desk.
