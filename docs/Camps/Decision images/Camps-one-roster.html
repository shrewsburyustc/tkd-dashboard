Camp page — one roster

Jeffrey, CP5 (2026-10-21): one roster instead of three tabs. Name (click → everything about the child) · Paid · then one column per camp day — tap a day to check the child in. Same data and rules as before; nothing in the database changes. Amber = what's new compared with the three tabs.

What it does

  • Summary line: today's attendance and who still has to pay — "9 of 15 here today · 5 to collect · $1,040".
  • Name (sorted A–Z): click → Camper detail (days, extras, parent / emergency phones, payments, Edit details, Withdraw …). A small amber note under the name only when it matters today: "Pick-up 3–4", "No pizza party".
  • Paid: Paid · $219 due · Owes $15 — tap one that isn't Paid → the Mark paid box (one click, this device's school, Undo).
  • Day columns (today's column tinted): ✓ 7:42 came · Check in signed up, tap to check in · ✗ didn't come (past day) · — not signed up. Tap a ✓ to undo it (toast with Undo). Check-in works on any day (v0.706).
  • Search finds a child fast. The possible-duplicate box and the Removed group stay at the bottom, as before.
  • Print roster prints this same grid.

Replaces

  • The three tabs Check in / Payments / Attendance (v0.704). Camps with more than 5 days: the day columns scroll sideways inside the roster on small screens, Name and Paid stay fixed.
