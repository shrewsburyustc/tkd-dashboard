A. Automatic — a child coming on a pizza day gets pizza and pays for it Recommended

Matches the paper forms ("Pizza party day $5 more", "Full week pizza parties included"). No box to tick: Mia picks Tue + Wed → Wed is a pizza day → $5 is added. Every child in the building on a pizza day eats.

Pros

  • Same as your paper form; nothing for parents to forget.
  • Pizza count = everyone there that day.

Cons

  • A child who doesn't want pizza still pays (staff can adjust with Edit details).

The pizza extra appears as a choice for the child's pizza days only. Parents can leave it off.
