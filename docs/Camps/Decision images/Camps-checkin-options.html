Camps roster — checking kids in when they arrive

Yes, every camp has a roster (C4). Picking a day shows exactly who is expected that day. What the approved design does not have yet is a way to mark a child as arrived. Two ways to add it — same roster, Mon, Apr 19, 9:05 AM. Amber = new.

A. Check in only — one tap when a child arrives Recommended

On a day's roster each child has a Check in button. One tap records the time (5-second Undo). The day line counts "8 of 14 here". Check-in is per day, so a child coming Mon + Wed is checked in on each day. Works on the desk PC and the mat-side tablet; staff and admins.

Pros

  • One tap per child at the door; you always know who's in the building.
  • "8 of 14 here" tells you at a glance who hasn't arrived.
  • Small to build: one time per child per day.

Cons

  • No record of who picked a child up or when they left.

B. Check in and check out — arrival and pick-up

Same Check in button; after it, the button becomes Check out. At pick-up staff tap it and type who picked the child up (e.g. "Mom", "Grandpa Joe"). The day line counts "8 here · 3 gone home · 3 not here yet".

Pros

  • Full day record: arrival, departure, who picked up.
  • At 3:30 you can see exactly who is still waiting.

Cons

  • Twice the taps per child per day, plus typing a name at the busiest time.
  • Bigger build (a dialog, more states, more tests).
  • "Picked up by" is typed by staff, not checked against an approved pick-up list.
