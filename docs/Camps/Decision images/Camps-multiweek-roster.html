Camp page — a camp longer than one week

Example: a 2-week camp, Mon, Jul 6 – Fri, Jul 17, 2026 (10 days). Today is Wed, Jul 15 (week 2). Summer weeks that are separate camps (v0.231) still show 5 days and never need this. Amber = the difference.

A. Week switcher — 5 days at a time Recommended

A "Week 1 · Week 2" switch appears above the roster only when the camp crosses more than one week. It opens on the current week. Each week shows at most 5 day columns, so it looks exactly like a normal one-week camp. Paid and "to collect" are for the whole camp; the "here today" count is today's.

Pros

  • Every camp looks the same: never more than 5 day columns.
  • Fits every phone and tablet; nothing slides sideways.
  • Opens on this week, which is what the door needs.

Cons

  • To see the whole camp at once you switch weeks (or print — Print roster prints all weeks).
  • One small extra control on longer camps.

B. Every day is a column — slide sideways on small screens

All 10 days are columns. On the PC they fit side by side (narrower cells). On tablets and phones the day columns slide sideways inside the roster; Name and Paid stay put.
