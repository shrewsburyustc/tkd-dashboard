Camps — question 3: how is the 8-week summer program set up?

Summer 2026 has 8 weeks (Jun 22 – Aug 21), each priced like April vacation (full week $275 member early, single days by the day grid). Left: the Camps list. Right: adding a camper who comes Week 2 (full week) and Week 3 (Tue + Thu). Amber = where the options differ.

A. One camp per week Recommended

Each summer week is its own camp, made from a "Summer week" template (only the dates change). The price grid is always rows 1–5 days. A family coming 2 weeks is added to 2 camps.

Each week's roster is exactly who's in the building that week.

Pros

  • Same screens as every other camp — nothing special for summer.
  • Grid stays small (5 rows).
  • Each week's roster and payments stand alone; a week can be changed or closed on its own.

Cons

  • A child doing 4 weeks is entered 4 times (parent details can be copied from their last entry).
  • 8 summer camps to create each year (from one template).
  • No "family's whole summer" total on one screen.

B. One summer camp with 8 weeks inside

One camp covers the whole summer. Its days are grouped into weeks, and the price grid is applied to each week separately. A family coming 2 weeks is one entry.

Pros

  • A child is entered once for the whole summer; one total for the family.
  • One camp to create each year.

Cons

  • New idea "weeks" everywhere: Add camp, the price math (grid per week), the roster (week filter), payments (paying week by week).
  • Add camper gets big: 40 day boxes, hard on a phone.
  • Only summer needs it — the other camps are 1–5 days.
