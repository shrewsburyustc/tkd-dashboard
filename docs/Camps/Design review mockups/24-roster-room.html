Camps roster: cards when the table has too little room (L-02)

The rule is already approved (v1.053, v1.130; Jeffrey 2026-11-05 and 2026-11-06): a roster picks its layout from the room beside the left panel, not from the window's width. Real preview screens of April Vacation Camp, a 5-day camp. Today the table keeps its columns whatever the room, so in a narrow room it is wider than its box and Friday and the ⋯ column can only be reached by scrolling sideways. After, when the room is too narrow for this week's columns, the roster draws its cards (the phone layout); a mouse also gets a ⋯ on each card. Amber = what differs.

How it decides. The table needs room for the page margins (50 px), the Camper and Paid columns, each day of the week on screen (about 89 px each) and, with a mouse, the ⋯ column (40 px). Measured on the built table. Room needed, by days in the shown week — 1 day 327 touch / 367 mouse · 2 416 / 456 · 3 505 / 545 · 4 594 / 634 · 5 683 / 723 · 6 772 / 812 · 7 861 / 901. The room is the window minus the left panel (72 px, 96 with Largest) and, on a PC, the scrollbar. So a 5-day camp with a mouse and the Standard panel switches to cards in windows under about 810 px; on a touch screen under about 755 px (about 779 with Largest). Also: the card layout has no legend under it (as phones today). Mouse sizes on cards are new: 32 px name link, 36 px day boxes, ⋯ as on the table; on touch they stay 44 px. No database change.
