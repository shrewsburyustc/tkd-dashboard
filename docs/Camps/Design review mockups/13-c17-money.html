Every difference between the two helpers (read side by side: Color Belt’s dollars, screens/testing/text.js:341, vs formatMoney, src/lib/money.js): (1) minus amounts — “$-335” → “−$335”: the only one Camps can show, and only on a reversal (the preview and the database store a reversal as minus cents; every other Camps amount is 0 or more: to collect uses max(0, owes), “Paid $75 more than the total” passes a plus amount). (2) thousands commas — the same: both write “$1,355”, “$10,000”. (3) zero — the same: “$0”. (4) cents — the same for whole cents (“$290.50”); Camps can’t have cents anyway (whole dollars only: price fields and Edit payment refuse cents). (5) not a whole number of cents (e.g. 29050.5) — dollars rounds it, formatMoney shows nothing; can’t happen (the database stores whole cents). (6) not a number (null, text) — both show nothing. (7) minus zero — dollars could write “$-0”, formatMoney “$0”; can’t happen. Not switched in this round: formatDollars on the public family pages (src/register/, the camp form), as the brief says.
