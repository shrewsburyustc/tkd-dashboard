Walkthrough picture 11b chose option B: Camps’ own four cards. Today a camp page has one summary line (“15 coming on Mon, Apr 19 · 6 to collect · $1,355 · Updated …”) that can’t be tapped. After: (1) four cards — Campers (everyone) · Coming [day] (filter) · To collect (filter; the number is campers who still owe, the money under it) · Payments recorded (a total, not a button). (2) “Coming” means: before the camp, its first day (“Coming Mon, Apr 19”); on a camp day, “Coming today” with “9 here” under it; between camp days, the next camp day; after the camp the card becomes Came (“9” / “of 9 days”, not a button). (3) Nobody owes: To collect 0, “All paid”. (4) The pizza count (an extra offered only on some days) moves to the cards’ own gray line under the row, only on that day. (5) Tapping Coming shows only that day’s campers, with “Showing coming today only · Show all”; To collect: “Showing to collect only · Show all”; the filter and Find a camper work together; in a two-week camp, if the Coming day is in the other week, the tap also moves the week switch to that week. (6) Hide private details on: only Campers and Coming. (7) “Updated …” on the right, above the cards. (8) Phones: two by two (the shared row does that itself).
