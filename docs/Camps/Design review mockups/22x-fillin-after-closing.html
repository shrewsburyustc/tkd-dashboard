Camps — Fill in: can staff use it after family sign-up has closed?

The camp page of a camp whose family sign-up is Closed (Veterans Day Camp), PC 1280. Fill in sits next to Registration link (on phones it is in the header ⋯). By default a camp's family sign-up closes the day before the first day, so a family that walks in on the first camp morning is after the closing date. Families online are not affected by this choice: their form stays closed either way. Amber = the part this choice decides.

A · only while sign-up is open
B · until the camp's last day (recommended)
C · after closing, admins only
Everything else about Fill in is the same in A, B and C (same form, same prices for today's date, same waiver and signature, duplicate check, “Entered by” you). The screens of Fill in itself come in the next picture.

Recommendation: B — the closing date exists for families online; the desk sees the child in front of them and can decide. If you sometimes close sign-up because a camp is full, pick C.
