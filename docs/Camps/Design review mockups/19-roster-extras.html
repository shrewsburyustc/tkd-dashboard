Camps — Early drop-off and Late pick-up on the roster (two ways to show them)

Jeffrey 2026-11-04: “We have Early Drop Off and Late Pick-Up always and we need to see on the roster.” Today the roster shows only one small note under a name, only on a camp day, and only the first extra (a child with both shows just one); there is no count of drop-offs or pick-ups, and before the camp nothing shows at all. Both options add (1) a count under the cards for the day the Coming card shows: “Mon, Apr 19 · Early drop-off: 5 · Late pick-up: 6” (on a camp day “Today · Early drop-off: 5 · Late pick-up: 6 · Pizza party: 11”, so today’s pizza line joins it). Option A (2) puts the extra’s first word, Early / Late, inside every signed-up day box, for every day of the week, and adds them to the legend under the grid (“· Early early drop-off · Late late pick-up”). Option B (2) writes the extras under the camper’s name, in full, for one day only (today on a camp day, else the Coming card’s day). The words come from each extra’s own name (the part before the comma), so a camp whose extras are named differently still works. Real preview data (April Vacation Camp). Amber = what changes.

Option A — in the day boxes (recommended)
Option B — under the name, one day
A — in the day boxes. Pros: you see every day at once (who stays late on Thursday, before the camp starts, and on the paper roster later if we want it); works the same in a two-week camp; the camp-day amber “Pick-up” note under the name goes away (a missing pizza party keeps its amber “No pizza”). Cons: each row is a bit taller; “Early” / “Late” are short words, explained in the legend under the grid. B — under the name. Pros: full words, no legend; rows only grow for campers with extras. Cons: only one day at a time (before the camp it shows the first day, so Thursday-only pick-ups are invisible until Thursday); the line can wrap on tablets. Same in both: no database change (each camper’s extras per day are already loaded); Hide private details keeps them (extras aren’t private); a printed roster isn’t part of this. Recommendation: A — the desk sees the whole week, which is what “always” needs.
How it was drawn: mock-x1-roster-extras.js adds the words on top of the real preview roster from each camper’s real extras; nothing in the app changed.
