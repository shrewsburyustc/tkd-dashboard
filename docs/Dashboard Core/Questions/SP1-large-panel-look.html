Side panel for the Grandmaster

Question 1 of 4: which look should the large panel have — A, B or C?

You asked (through Main): “a side-panel toggle for a large panel with names under the icons, for the Grandmaster”. Today on PC (1024 px and wider, with a mouse) the side panel shows icons only: a name shows only while the mouse points at an icon or the keyboard is on it (rulebook v0.4). Tablets and phones already show the names, at 12 px. A PC window narrower than 1024 px (for example a 1280 screen zoomed to 150%) already gets the tablet panel. Which device the Grandmaster uses isn’t recorded anywhere.

The switch turns the large panel on. Where it lives, how it’s remembered and who can use it are the next questions, one at a time. Pictures: Home in the preview (fake data), each option’s look put on the real page. Amber box = what the option changes.

A — the tablet’s panel on PC (PC only)

72 px wide · 20 px icons · 12 px names · buttons 64×56 · needs a window 492 px tall

  • A look that is already approved (rulebook §1.3, the Home mockups).
  • Bigger buttons on PC: 64×56 instead of 36×36.
  • The page loses 20 px on PC; tablets don’t change.
  • Names stay at 12 px, the rulebook’s smallest text size.
  • Nothing changes on tablets: if he uses the mat-side tablet, A does nothing for him.

B — the same panel, bigger names (PC and tablet)

72 px wide · 20 px icons · 14 px names · buttons 64×58 · needs a window 510 px tall

  • Bigger names: 12 → 14 px, the size of body text, on PC and on the mat-side tablet.
  • Same width as today’s tablet panel: the page loses 20 px on PC and nothing on tablets.
  • Icons stay in the rulebook’s 18–20 px.
  • A tight fit: “Prospects” fills 60 of the button’s 64 px; a longer section name added later would need a wider panel.
  • Icons and buttons are no bigger than A’s.

C — a bigger panel (PC and tablet)

96 px wide · 24 px icons (the largest the rulebook allows) · 14 px names · buttons 88×64 · needs a window 556 px tall

  • The biggest icons and buttons, on PC and on the mat-side tablet; names as big as B’s.
  • The page is 44 px narrower than today on PC and 24 px narrower on tablets.
  • The tallest: in a window under 556 px (for example a 1366×768 laptop zoomed to 125%) the last name, Finance, starts off-screen and the whole page scrolls a little. A and B fit there.
  • 24 px icons need a rulebook exception (the panel’s icons are 18–20 px).

All three: the switch needs a rulebook row (it changes §1.3’s PC row, and for B and C its tablet row too; the name bubble isn’t needed while the names show). Pointing with the mouse greys the icon’s pill, as §2.3 says, not the whole button. Phones don’t change. No screen scrolls sideways with any of them (all 19 screens checked at 640, 820 and 1024 px).

Recommendation: B. Bigger names help a reader most, and B gives them on PC and on the mat-side tablet, for 20 px of page width on PC, nothing on tablets, and no new icon size. Reading “large” as “easier to read” is my assumption, from the design review’s checklist, written with him in mind: “is it big enough on the mat-side tablet?”. If “large” only means “the panel with names”, A is the smaller change; if the icons are too small for him as well, C.
