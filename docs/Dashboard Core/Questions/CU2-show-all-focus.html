Cleanup 2: Show all with the keyboard, where the focus goes

The shared count cards (parts page, M6), PC 1280×800. Keyboard only: Tab to Not paid, Enter (the filter turns on and the gray line shows “Showing not paid only · Show all”), Tab to Show all, Enter. The amber outline marks Not paid in both pictures.

Today

Show all disappears as it’s pressed, and the focus falls to the page. No ring anywhere, and the next Tab starts again at the top of the page.

With the fix

The focus goes back to the card that was on: the blue ring is on Not paid. The next Tab carries on from there, and Enter turns the filter back on.

Mouse and touch: nothing you can see changes. The look of the cards and the gray line doesn’t change either; only where the keyboard focus lands.
