D6 · The receipt's school line and Balance line: weight 600 or 500

The shared receipt (M4, v1.105) draws two lines heavier than its other words: the school's name at the top and the Balance line (outlined in amber). As drawn and as built they are weight 600 (“semibold”), copied from Black Belt's receipt. §7 of the rulebook allows only 400 (regular) and 500 (medium). The design review flagged the mismatch. The receipt's other dark words (the student and event details, the table headings) are already 500.

Measured on this PC (Windows 11, Chrome, the app's own font list): Segoe UI has no medium style, so Chrome draws 500 with its semibold, the same as 600. The receipt at 500 and at 600 is pixel-identical: the printed page, the box on screen and both lines, compared pixel by pixel. Apple's font has a real medium, so there 500 is a shade lighter. That can't be pictured from this PC, and other browsers (Firefox) weren't measured.

Recommendation: A. It follows the rulebook, and receipts printed from Chrome on the school PCs don't change at all.
