R2-05 · An Undo pressed while another save runs waits its turn

Today: press Undo while another change is still saving, and the toast vanishes and nothing happens. The change stays, and nothing says so (Camps: withdraw Zoe Park, withdraw Sofia, press Zoe's Undo during Sofia's save: both stay withdrawn).

With R2-05: the Undo waits its turn and runs as soon as that save ends. Meanwhile its toast stays and says "Undoing…" in the busy look the "Are you sure?" box's busy button uses (v1.008): grayed words and the busy cursor, still reachable with the keyboard. When it's done: "Undone" for 5 seconds. (Every Undo shows "Undoing…" while it runs; a quick one barely flashes it.) Camps → Thanksgiving Camp in the preview; the amber outline marks the toast.
