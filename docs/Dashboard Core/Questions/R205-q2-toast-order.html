R2-05, question 2 · When an Undo waits for another save, what happens to that save's own toast?

The example (Camps → Thanksgiving Camp, PC): Zoe Park is withdrawn, and her toast has Undo. Then Sofia Rivera is withdrawn, and on a slow connection her save is still running when Zoe's Undo is pressed. Zoe's Undo waits for Sofia's save; its toast says "Undoing…" (question 1, answered A). When Sofia's save ends, it has its own toast, "Sofia Rivera withdrawn · Undo". Only one toast shows at a time. In what order?

When an Undo waits for another save, what happens to that save's own toast?

A · Sofia's toast right after Zoe's Undo is done (my pick)
Zoe withdrawn
→Undo pressed while Sofia saves
Waits, then runs
→Zoe’s Undo done
Sofia’s toast, its full 5 seconds
+ Nothing is lost: Sofia's Undo comes as soon as Zoe's is done.
− In this one case there's no "Undone" toast; Zoe is back on the page, which shows it worked.
B · "Undone" first, then Sofia's toast
Waits, then runs
→Zoe’s Undo done
"Undone", 2 seconds
→then
Sofia’s toast, its 5 seconds
+ Both messages are seen.
− Sofia's Undo comes 2 seconds later, and "Undone" gets less than a toast's usual 5 seconds.
C · Sofia's toast as soon as her save ends
Waits
→Sofia’s save ends
Sofia’s toast; Zoe’s Undo runs behind it
+ Sofia's Undo shows soonest.
− "Undoing…" goes before Zoe's Undo is done, and nothing says it worked (if it fails, its amber bar says so).
