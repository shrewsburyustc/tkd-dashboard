M2 · The shared “are you sure?” box — mockup

One box for every section (rulebook v1.008 with D1a–D1d and D10a). The button that changes nothing on the left, saying what stays; the action on the right repeating the words pressed — red when it loses something for good. Amber outline = what the shared box decides. The 320 column shows the stacked buttons (D1b) and, at 640 tall, no pinned button row.

1Loses something for good (Delete permanently): “Keep student” on the left with the first focus (blue ring); the action on the right in red, repeating the menu's words. The section writes the question, the sentence and the gray hint.

2While it works (D1a): the pressed button grays and says what it is doing — its verb + “ing…” (“Deleting…”, “Undoing…”) — and “Keep student” grays too (new: today only blue and red buttons have this gray look). Esc, a click outside and Back wait until it's done. Both stay focusable for the keyboard. The buttons never move: whether they stack is decided from their usual words, so a stacked pair stays stacked (320).

3If it fails: the box stays open; the section's amber note appears under the buttons, so they don't move (v0.81), and stays until the box closes (v1.004). Pressing the action again tries again.

4Loses something, but not for good (Undo payment — both stay in the history; Color Belt's Cancel private testing, which an admin can restore): the action is blue; the first focus stays on the button that changes nothing. (“Keep it” where there's no short name.)

5Loses nothing (Prospects' “Came?” when a card is dropped on Came; Forms' kiosk asks): the action is blue and has the first focus; the other button still says what stays — “Keep in Booked”, the card's column (D1d). “Cancel” is never a confirm-box button; it stays in form dialogs.

6The leave warnings use the same box (D1c — they swap): “Stay on this page” on the left, focused first; “Leave anyway” on the right in red, because leaving loses the unsaved results for good. Leaving happens at once, so there's no “…ing” step.

7Black Belt's “Discard changes?” (D10a) follows the same rule: “Keep editing” on the left, focused first; Discard on the right in red.
