Q20 · Close the day: how is a refund to a card counted?

Black Belt is the first section that can refund to a card (Star refunds only by cash or check). Example day: $400.00 paid by card, and $150.00 refunded to a card. Close the day compares the “Card machine total” with what the dashboard expects. The amber line is what changes. To decide, look at your card machine's end-of-day report: is its total before or after refunds?

A · Card total = money in; the refund is a separate line

What it does: The expected card figure is sales only: the $400 card payment. The $150 card refund is listed under it, so whoever closes knows it was there.

When it's right: Right if your card machine's day report shows sales and refunds separately (gross sales).

Risk: If the machine's total is after refunds ($250), every day with a card refund shows “Short $150”.

B · Card total = money in minus card refunds (like cash today) Recommended

Close the day

Count the drawer and type what you find. The dashboard compares it with this day's payments.

$ 1,115.00
Checks in the drawer
Expected 2 checks, $3,600.00
$ 3,600.00
Card machine total
Expected $250.00 ($400.00 in − $150.00 refunded) · from the machine's day report
$ ____

What it does: The expected card figure is after refunds: $400 − $150 = $250, written the same way cash already is (“Expected $300.00 ($320.00 in − $20.00 refunded)”, v0.209). In the “In groups” layout the Card card shows $250.00, as Cash and Check do today.

When it's right: Right if your card machine's day report total is after refunds — most card terminals' batch / settlement total is. Same logic and wording as cash refunds.

Risk: If your machine shows gross sales, every day with a card refund shows “Over $150”.

Either way: the Refunds group header lists the card part (“card −$150.00”), the Total stays after all refunds, and nothing else in Close the day changes.
