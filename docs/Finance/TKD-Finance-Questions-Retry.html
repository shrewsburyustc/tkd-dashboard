Finance: two small questions about the word “retry”

Both are optional polish. Nothing is broken and nothing is waiting on them. Green = my recommendation. Amber outline = the one thing that differs between the options.

Question 1

When closing the day fails, should the amber bar get its own Retry button?

How often: rarely. The bar below only appears when the Close the day box has already closed and the database then answers something unexpected. Your count is kept in both options; nothing you typed is lost.

A · Leave it as it is (recommended, also Main’s pick)

The bar tells you what to do; you press the same button again

What you see on the Daily report

⚠Couldn’t close the day. Nothing you typed is lost. Check the connection, then retry.✕

What you do

Close the day…→ Box opens, your count still typed in→ Retry close the day
  • Good: nothing to build, nothing new to test.
  • Good: the same two taps as option B.
  • Not so good: the bar says “retry” but has no button on it, so you have to know to press Close the day again.
B · Add a Retry button to the bar

The button on the bar opens the box for you

What you see on the Daily report

⚠Couldn’t close the day. Nothing you typed is lost. Check the connection, then retry.↻ Retry✕

What you do

↻ Retry→ Box opens, your count still typed in→ Retry close the day
  • Good: the bar explains itself: the word “retry” sits next to a Retry button.
  • Not so good: still two taps: it cannot close the day by itself, because the box must check your count first. It only saves you from having to know where to press.
  • Not so good: a small change in Finance’s screens and its tests.
Question 2

Should Black Belt and Star also say “retry” instead of “try again”?

Why it comes up: Finance now says “… Check the connection, then retry.” (your “Yes, small batch”). Black Belt and Star’s staff screens still say “… then try again.” right next to a button labelled Retry. Words only: no database change, no new screens.

A · Yes, align them (recommended)

Same words in every section

Example: Black Belt, Results, when the CSV fails

⚠Couldn’t download the CSV. Check the connection, then retry.↻ Retry✕

Today: “… Check the connection, then try again.” beside the same Retry button.

Example: Star, saving grades

⚠Couldn’t save the grades. Check the connection, then retry.↻ Retry✕
  • Good: one wording across the dashboard, as the rulebook’s shared sentences (v1.051) say; the sentence matches the button.
  • Cost: about 20 sentences in Black Belt’s staff screens, about 16 in Star’s, and 2 shared dialogs (Save failed, Delete failed). Each section’s own session does its part in its next batch. Words only; their word checks get updated.
B · Leave them as they are

Finance says “retry”, the others keep “try again”

Example: Black Belt, Results, when the CSV fails

⚠Couldn’t download the CSV. Check the connection, then try again.↻ Retry✕

The sentence says “try again”, the button says “Retry”: both mean the same thing.

Finance, for comparison (already live)

⚠Couldn’t reopen the day. Check the connection, then retry.↻ Retry✕
  • Good: no work, nothing to test, no risk.
  • Not so good: two wordings for the same failure depending on the section. It is the kind of small inconsistency the design review was set up to remove.
  • Not part of this question: the family sign-up forms keep “try again” on purpose, next to a “Try again” button.
To answer, just say: “Question 1: A” or “B”, and “Question 2: align” or “leave”. If you say nothing, both stay as they are today (Question 1 = A, Question 2 = leave).
