Proposed (option B) · the button says what stays
The rule in B: every “are you sure?” box — including the ones that lose nothing — names what stays: “Keep …” for a thing, “Stay …” for a place. “Cancel” stays the word in form dialogs, where it throws away the typing (Cancel · Save). The first focus stays as decided in D1c: on the action in these four, since nothing is lost. Pictures 5–8 are drawn over the real boxes; nothing in the app was changed.
