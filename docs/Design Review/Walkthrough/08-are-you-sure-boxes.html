Item 8 · “Are you sure?” boxes: “Keep …” or “Cancel”

Approved in each section, plus some drift. Every box that asks before something can't be undone (rule 1.4) has one button that changes nothing. Its words were decided section by section, and no rule says which to use: “Keep student / Keep payment / Keep candidate” in most roster boxes (Color Belt, Star, Black Belt), “Cancel” in the settings-type boxes (Color Belt classes and ranks, Star categories, Schedule) and in Black Belt's cycle and Undo payment boxes.
