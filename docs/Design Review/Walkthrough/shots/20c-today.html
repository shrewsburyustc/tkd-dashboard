Rule §4 today: “Target: WCAG 2.1 AA”

Written in v0.2 (2026-10-15). WCAG = the web's accessibility checklist; AA = the level most laws and schools ask for.

  • Every icon-only button has a hidden name that matches its tooltip
  • A visible 2px blue focus ring, never removed
  • Text contrast at least 4.5 : 1; icons and control borders at least 3 : 1 (v0.26)
  • Meaning is never shown by color alone
  • Plus the touch rule elsewhere: 44px tap areas on touch screens (§2.5)
