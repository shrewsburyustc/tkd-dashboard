ASame as Star (recommended)

Pros
  • Staff already learn these rules for Star and Color Belt: one set of rules everywhere.
  • The front desk can take a check when no admin is in; every payment is logged with who and when.
  • Money mistakes after the 5-second Undo, refunds and bounced checks stay with admins.
Cons
  • Black Belt amounts are big ($870–$2,700) and anyone at the desk can record them.
  • Moving a testing day (e.g. for rain) needs an admin.

BMoney: admins only

Pros
  • Tightest control of large amounts and of post-dated checks made out to Master Jeong.
Cons
  • A family handing in checks when no admin is there: staff can't record them, so checks sit unrecorded.
  • Different from Star and Color Belt, so staff must remember which testing allows what.

CStaff do more

Pros
  • Fewer "ask an admin" moments: staff can move a day, reopen registration late and fix payment mistakes.
Cons
  • Deadlines and the money record can be changed at the desk, which is easier to get wrong and harder to trace to one person while the desk account logs as "Staff".
  • Different from Star and Color Belt.
