Black Belt testing · Cycles — mockups (draft for review)

Jae H. Jeong's U.S. Taekwondo Center · Black Belt testing, registration track · guidelines v0.220 · October 18, 2026 · Draft — not approved yet

Design only — not a working app. Nothing here is built or deployed. Each screen is shown at PC (1280px, mouse), tablet (820px, touch) and phone (390px, touch); a few are also drawn 320px wide to show nothing scrolls sideways. Names, dates, counts, prices, the links and the QR codes are examples (the QR codes are not scannable). Unless a caption says otherwise, “today” is Mon, Feb 15, 2027. Amber text = proposed here, not decided yet.

The dashboard side of Black Belt testing registration: where it sits in the Testing chooser, the Cycles tab, Add cycle with its list of testing days, deadlines and payment due dates, the Rubric and prices tab (payment plans, Kukkiwon fees, waiver), the Registration links dialog and the cycle page. The public form is in TKD-BlackBelt-Form-Mockups.html; the Candidates tab and payments are in TKD-BlackBelt-Candidates-Mockups.html. The rubric editor, Grading and Results are designed with grading.

Already decided — drawn this way below

  1. Where it lives (v0.59, v0.60, status B2): the third row of the Testing chooser; Black Belt home has two tabs, Cycles and Rubric and prices; a cycle's page has Candidates · Grading · Results. Joint for both schools (v0.204).
  2. A cycle holds a list of testing days (v0.209): any number; each has a name, date, time, place and the parts graded that day (Physical, Technical, Curriculum); + Add testing day; the cycle also holds the deadlines (application due, submissions due) and the payment due dates. Add cycle starts as a copy of the last cycle.
  3. Due dates for each plan's payments (v0.217): payment plans live on Rubric and prices (In full · 1 payment, 3 payments, + Add plan), each with an amount per payment for each Dan, plus a Kukkiwon fee per Dan (added to payment 1). A new cycle copies them; its own amounts can change only until someone has paid.
  4. Roles (v0.210): admins add and edit cycles and edit Rubric and prices; staff view Rubric and prices (“View only. Ask an admin to make changes.”), close registration any time and open it only before the closing date.
  5. Registration (v0.85, v0.212): Open / Closed switch; the form closes by itself at the end of the application date (11:59 PM Massachusetts time); only admins reopen after it.
  6. Links (v0.90, v0.98): one link and QR code per school; the link sets the candidate's school.
  7. Waiver (v0.213): admins edit the waiver text on Rubric and prices; a change makes a new version and old registrations keep theirs; signed waivers are kept a set number of years (asked of the insurer), then deleted.

Proposals in this file (amber below)

  1. Testing chooser icon for Black Belt testing: ti-award (a medal); the next-cycle line shows the cycle's next testing day (“Next: Apr 3 · both schools”, later “Next: May 1 · both schools”).
  2. Cycle tags: Not open yet → Open until Mar 27 → Registration closed → Next day May 1 (between testing days) → Testing today (on a testing day); the cycle moves to Past the day after its last testing day.
  3. Row ⋯ on Cycles: Copy Worcester link · Copy Shrewsbury link · Close / Open registration · then Delete cycle (admins, red), like Star v0.91, but only while no payment is recorded; after that it is grayed out with the reason.
  4. Empty states: “No Black Belt testing cycles yet.” (admin) / “No Black Belt testing cycles yet. An admin adds them.” (staff) / “No upcoming cycles.” / “No past cycles yet.” Fall 2026 finishes in the old system, so Past starts empty.
  5. The very first cycle copies a starting schedule loaded once at setup from the Sheet's Fall 2026 “Testing Info” (days, times, places, deadlines, due dates); the hint says “Copied from Fall 2026.” Copied dates are kept as they were until changed.
  6. Payment 1 of every plan starts on the Application due date and follows it until changed by hand (like Star's closing date). The Kukkiwon fee is due with payment 1 (v0.217).
  7. Each plan in Add cycle has an Offer this plan tick (on by default), so a season can leave a plan out without changing Rubric and prices (v0.217 detail 2).
  8. The testing day dialog opens on top of Add cycle on PC and tablet (the cycle dialog stays dimmed behind it), and as a second full-screen page with ← on phones.
  9. Place is a dropdown: Worcester school · Shrewsbury school · Other place (then a text field). The second part has its own start and end time and place.
  10. v0.209 detail 1: Save is refused while a part is graded on no day: “Curriculum isn't graded on any testing day. Tick it on a day, or add a testing day.” Save stays gray.
  11. v0.209 detail 2: a part may be ticked on two days (e.g. Curriculum day 1 and day 2); the hint says “Grades from both days count as one.”
  12. v0.209 detail 3: an outdoor part is a Second part of the same day (its own time and place), not a day of its own.
  13. v0.209 detail 4: a day that has grades can't be deleted; its Delete icon is grayed out and tapping it shows “Physical has grades, so it can't be deleted. Change its date, time or place instead.” Untick a part on that day is refused the same way.
  14. Payment plans on Rubric and prices: one line per plan with its amounts in the summary; a plan opens to show its payments per Dan and the total. Plans are listed in the order they appear on the family form; that order is changed by dragging the grip (v0.62) (v0.217 detail 4).
  15. Waiver version numbers are the year and a count (“2027-1”, “2027-2”); Edit opens the text and Save makes the next version.
  16. Waiver keeping: “Keep signed waivers for [3] years after the last testing day. For a candidate under 18: until 3 years after they turn 18, if that's later.” The number is a placeholder until the insurer answers (v0.213).
  17. Registration links dialog: the Star dialog without Print flyer (a Black Belt flyer wasn't asked for); a line on preselecting a Dan with ?dan=1…?dan=4 (v0.211).
  18. Cycle page header: the summary is “First day Sat, Apr 3, 2027 · 3 testing days · both schools · 9 candidates”; the header has a ⋯ menu (Edit cycle for admins, Registration links, Close / Open registration) instead of Star's outline Registration link button.

1. Testing chooser

The Testing rail icon. Black Belt testing is the third row of the choice page and of the hover menu (v0.59).

What it does

  • Click or tap the Testing icon → the Testing choice page: one row per testing type, each with its next event line and ti-chevron-right. Black Belt testing is the third row.
  • PC only: hovering the Testing icon (150ms) opens the dark menu beside it; clicking a name goes straight there. Drawn while on Black Belt testing, so it is highlighted.
  • Black Belt's line: “Next: Apr 3 · both schools”, the cycle's next testing day; once Physical is done it reads “Next: May 1 · both schools”. No line before the first cycle exists (v0.59).
  • Icon: ti-award (a medal), proposed. Color belt keeps ti-certificate, Star keeps ti-star.

Rules applied

  • v0.59 — choice page + hover menu (mouse devices only), “← Testing” back link inside each type; touch devices never see the menu.
  • 2.3 / 2.4 — one icon per meaning; a new icon is added to the master icon map when approved.
  • 1.3 — same rows on every device; only the size changes.

2. Cycles tab (admin)

What an admin sees when opening Black Belt testing. Upcoming is selected; soonest first. Today: Mon, Feb 15, 2027.

What it does

  • One row per cycle: cycle name, first testing day, candidates (both schools), and one status tag. Spring 2027 is open until its application date, Mar 27 (green); Fall 2027 is saved but not open (gray).
  • Click or tap a row to open the cycle page (screen 8). Edit opens Edit cycle (same as Add cycle, screen 4); ⋯ holds the rest.
  • Tags after the application date: Registration closed → “Next day May 1” between testing days → “Testing today” on a testing day; the day after the last testing day the cycle moves to Past.
  • ⋯ (admin): Copy Worcester link · Copy Shrewsbury link · Close registration · Delete cycle (red). Delete is allowed only while no payment is recorded; then it is grayed out: “Payments are recorded for this cycle.”
  • Phone: one stacked line per cycle with ⋯; Edit cycle moves into ⋯ (rule 1.1).

Rules applied

  • v0.60 / v0.89 — Star's page pattern: Upcoming / Past switch (gray selected side), rows, “+ Add cycle” as the page-header button (v0.45). N opens Add cycle on PC.
  • v0.85 / v0.212 — registration closes by itself at the end of the application date; the tag shows “Open until [date]”.
  • 8 — one tag per row; 2.5 — touch rows 48px+, max 2 visible row actions; 1.4 — Close registration happens at once with a 5-second Undo.

3. Cycles tab — staff view and empty states

Signed in as the front-desk account (role Staff), and the tab before any cycle exists.

What it does

  • Staff: no Add cycle, no Edit icons; the gray line says what staff can do. Staff open cycle pages and copy links like admins.
  • Before the application date staff can close and reopen registration. After it, “Open registration” is grayed out with the reason (drawn on the phone, Mon, Mar 29).

Rules applied

  • v0.86 / v0.210 — staff close any time, open only before the closing date; admins reopen after it. Enforced in the database, not just hidden.
  • 8 Empty states — one line, plus the action when there is one.
  • v0.66 — grayed-out menu item with its reason under it.

4. Add cycle

“+ Add cycle” (or N on PC). PC and tablet: a dialog over the dimmed tab. Phone: full screen. Drawn: adding Spring 2027, the very first cycle, so the tab behind is still empty.

What it does

  • Cycle name, then the Testing days list: each day shows its name, date, time, place, the second part when it has one, and what is graded that day. Edit and Delete on each day; + Add testing day under the list (screen 5).
  • Deadlines: Application due (also the day registration closes) and Submissions due (hand-in work).
  • Payment due dates, one small group per payment plan from Rubric and prices: In full · 1 payment (Mar 27); 3 payments (Mar 27 · Apr 24 · May 29); 2 payments (Mar 27 · May 29).
  • Registration Open / Closed (new cycles start Closed) with “Close automatically at the end of Application due”. The date isn't typed twice: it is the Application due date.
  • Starts as a copy of the last cycle's days, deadlines and due dates; usually only the dates change. The very first cycle copies Fall 2026's schedule, loaded once at setup from the Sheet.
  • Each plan has an “Offer this plan” tick, so a season can leave a plan off the family form (v0.217 detail 2). Payment 1 follows Application due until changed by hand.
  • Edit cycle (row Edit icon, or ⋯ on the cycle page) shows the same fields with “Last changed by …” (drawn in screen 5).
  • Unsaved changes: Esc, clicking outside or ← keeps the dialog open with “Unsaved changes. Save or cancel first.”; phones add Discard changes (v0.46, v0.56).

Rules applied

  • v0.209 — list of testing days with the parts graded each day; deadlines; due dates; copied from the last cycle. v0.217 — due dates for each plan's payments.
  • v0.85 / v0.212 — Registration switch and the automatic close at 11:59 PM Massachusetts time on the application date.
  • v0.210 — admins only. 8 Forms — labels above fields, 32px fields (36px on touch), errors under the field. 3.5 — a failed save keeps the dialog open with the error banner and Retry.
  • Toast after Save: “Cycle added” / “Changes saved”.

5. Add or edit a testing day — and the four v0.209 details

The Edit icon on a day, or “+ Add testing day”, inside Add cycle / Edit cycle. Then the error and the grayed Delete.

What it does

  • Name (what families see), Date, Start time and End time (15-minute steps, the v0.51 dropdown), Place, an optional second part, and Graded this day: Physical, Technical, Curriculum (tick one or more).
  • Detail 3 · the outdoor part is a second part of the same day: its own start and end time and place (Horgan Arena, Auburn). It shows as one day on the form and on grading.
  • Detail 2 · a part may be graded on two days (drawn on the tablet: Curriculum day 2); the hint says grades from both days count as one.
  • Detail 1 · Add cycle refuses Save while a part is graded on no day; the error sits under the Testing days list and Save is gray (PC, below).
  • Detail 4 · a day that has grades can't be deleted: its Delete icon is grayed out, and pressing it shows the reason under the day (tablet and phone, below). Unticking a part that already has grades on that day is refused the same way.
  • The day dialog opens on top of Add cycle (PC, tablet); on phones it is a second full-screen page whose ← goes back to Add cycle. Save returns to the cycle with the day updated; nothing is saved to the database until the cycle's Save.

Rules applied

  • v0.209 — name, date, time, place and parts per day; the four details are settled here.
  • v0.51 — custom dropdowns; v0.46 / v0.56 — unsaved changes keep the dialog open.
  • 8 Forms — validate as you type; errors under the field in amber, Save gray while there is an error (v0.57).
  • 2.5 — touch rows 56px with two 44px icon buttons (Edit, Delete).

6. Rubric and prices

The second tab of Black Belt home. Two sections for now: Payment plans (with the Kukkiwon fee) and Waiver.

What it does

  • Payment plans: one line per plan (In full · 1 payment; 3 payments; 2 payments) with its amounts in the summary. A plan opens to show its payments per Dan and the total; + Add plan adds another (e.g. 2 payments, Monthly 4). The Edit icon opens Edit plan (name, number of payments, amount per payment for each Dan). The order is the order on the family form, changed with the grip (v0.62). Edit plan isn't drawn in this file.
  • Kukkiwon certificate fee per Dan, optional per candidate, added to payment 1.
  • Waiver: the text (first lines, Read all opens it), its version, Edit, and who signs. Edit opens the text; Save makes a new version (tablet, below). Version names: year and count, “2027-1”.
  • Keep signed waivers for [3] years after the last testing day (and for under-18s until 3 years after they turn 18, if later): the number waits for the insurer's answer (v0.213).
  • The rubric (parts, subjects, credits, scoring tables, pass marks) is also kept on this tab. It is designed together with grading, so it isn't drawn here and the app shows no placeholder for it until then (P7).
  • Staff see the same tab with “View only. Ask an admin to make changes.”: no grip, no Edit icons, no + Add plan, no waiver Edit; plans still open and close.

Rules applied

  • v0.217 — any number of plans, each with its payments and amounts per Dan; Kukkiwon fee per Dan; cycles copy them; recorded amounts never change.
  • v0.213 — versioned waiver text edited by admins; signatures kept a set number of years, then deleted.
  • v0.210 / v0.61 — admins edit, staff view only, enforced in the database.
  • Star Rubrics and prices pattern (blocks, Edit icon on a block head, “Last changed by”). Toasts: “Plan saved”, “Kukkiwon fees saved”, “Waiver saved · version 2027-2”.
