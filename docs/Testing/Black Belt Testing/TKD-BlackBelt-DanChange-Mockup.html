Black Belt · changing a candidate's Dan after grading started (Q-G4, v0.951)

In a candidate's Edit details, staff change Alex Kim from 1st Dan to 2nd Dan and press Save. He already has grades on the 1st Dan subjects, so the dashboard asks first. Same wording and look as Star's (v0.164); the count includes Black Belt's extra-points counts and chosen make-up subjects when there are any.

  • 1Wording: title "Change Alex Kim to 2nd Dan?" · body "The 9 grades entered for 1st Dan subjects will be cleared. 2nd Dan has different subjects. This can't be undone." When there are extra-points counts too: "The 9 grades and 4 extra-point counts entered for 1st Dan will be cleared." Chosen make-up subjects (admins) are named the same way.
  • 2Buttons: Cancel (back to Edit details, nothing saved) · red "Change Dan and clear 9 grades" (red = it deletes, rule §7). The cleared grades stay in the change log.
  • 3Sizes: a centred dialog on PC and tablet (44px buttons on touch); full screen on phones with the buttons stacked, the red one on top.
  • 4Only when needed: no grades for the old Dan → Save just saves, no question. A make-up row has no Dan field to change (Q-G4). A date-of-birth fix never asks — the result is worked out again.
  • 5If something changed meanwhile (someone graded another subject while the question was open), Save is refused with "Alex Kim's grades changed. Check the number and try again." and the question opens again with the new count.
