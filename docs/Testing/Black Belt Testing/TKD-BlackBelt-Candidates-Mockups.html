Design only — not a working app. Names, dates, emails and amounts are made up. Every screen is shown at PC (1280px, mouse), tablet (820px, touch) and phone (390px, touch); the main phone screens also at 320px. Text in amber is proposed and still to confirm. “Today” is Mon, Apr 26, 2027 unless a screen says otherwise.

Decided — drawn this way below

  1. Roles as Star (v0.210): staff record payments (5-second Undo), add candidates, fix details, change the plan or Kukkiwon before anything is paid, print receipts; admins also edit, undo, refund or mark a check returned after the toast, change the plan after a payment, and remove candidates. Enforced in the database.
  2. Open form; registrations count at once; possible duplicates flagged; staff add late families at the desk (v0.212).
  3. Waiver: signer, date and version kept, “on file”; only admins open a signature; families who can’t sign online sign on paper and staff tick “signed on paper” (v0.213).
  4. Payment plans per cycle (In full, 3 payments, any other, e.g. 2 payments), amounts per Dan, Kukkiwon fee with payment 1 (v0.217).
  5. Post-dated checks: recorded when handed in, each counts as paid that day; Record payment has Amount, Method, For, optional Note; no check date or number (v0.218).
  6. One Payments column: Paid in full / Next $X by date / Late · $X since date / Not paid, with the balance; four summary cards that filter; phone rows with filter pills (v0.219).
  7. Print receipt: one page with the full history, totals, both schools, “Printed … by …”, “Fees are non-refundable.” (v0.220).
  8. From Star: cards in §8 style, possible-duplicate box, detail view opens as a summary, follows the location switcher, Add candidate in the page header (v0.114, v0.115, v0.112, v0.118, v0.119).

3. Candidate detail (desk device)

Clicking Alex Kim opens a read-only summary (Star v0.112): a centered dialog on PC and tablet, full screen on the phone. Alex’s family handed in all three checks on Mar 27 (screen 4).

  • Candidate: name, date of birth with the age on the first testing day, email for results, school, Dan. Date of birth and email appear only here, and only on desk devices (§5, v0.210).
  • Registration: date and source (family form, or desk), reference BB-####, and the waiver line “Signed by Jordan Kim (parent) · Mar 10 · version 2027-1 · on file” (v0.213). Admins also get View signature; staff don’t (v0.210).
  • Payments: the plan with its amounts and due dates, Kukkiwon, then every payment: date, for, how, amount, note, recorded by (v0.219). Totals: total due, paid, balance, next due.
  • Buttons: Record payment (screen 4), Print receipt (screen 8), Close, and ⋯ with Edit details, Change plan or Kukkiwon, and Remove candidate (admins). Edit details works as in Star (fields, Save and Cancel, unsaved-changes guard; v0.112, v0.46).
  • Proposed: Record payment stays available on a paid-in-full candidate (for an extra or mistaken payment, which then shows as overpaid, screen 9). Remove candidate opens Star’s two choices, Withdraw (kept under Removed, can be restored) or Delete permanently (v0.113). A History section from the change log (Star) is left out here to keep the view short; the log still records everything.
