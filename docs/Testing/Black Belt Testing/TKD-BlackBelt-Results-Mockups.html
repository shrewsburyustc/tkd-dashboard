Jae H. Jeong's U.S. Taekwondo Center · Black Belt testing · a cycle’s Results tab · rulebook v0.946 (G9) · October 23, 2026 · Draft — not approved yet

Design only — not a working app. Nothing here is built or deployed. Each screen is drawn at PC (1280px, mouse), tablet upright (820 × 1180, touch), phone (390 × 844, touch) and 320px wide, except the printed sheet (paper). Parts with a dashed amber outline and a “Proposed: see N” label are proposals still to confirm; each number is listed under its screen and at the end. All names, emails, dates of birth and scores are made up; the pass marks are the real 1st–4th Dan ones from the rubric.

Decision G9 (v0.946) gives each cycle a Results tab that copies Star testing’s Results whole: one Dan at a time, a result per candidate with its reason, Email result with Mark as sent, result email texts that admins edit, a printed results sheet and an admin CSV. This file draws the Black Belt version, built on the approved Candidates mockups (page frame) and Star’s approved Results mockups (look and states).

Already decided — drawn this way below

  1. G1 (v0.938): Passed = the Total reaches the pass mark of the candidate’s age bracket and no D. D → Make-up · D in [subject]; below the mark with no D → Make-up · below [mark]; grades missing → Not finished. Worked out by the database; no hand override; results never lock.
  2. G2 (v0.939): an Other requirement with no grade after the hand-in date shows Late, and the result reads Not finished · [subject] late (due [date]). Nothing becomes D by itself; nothing is sent to families automatically.
  3. G3 (v0.940): D subjects are retested; below the mark with no D → “Choose subjects” (admins), lowest grades ticked to start. G4 (v0.941): make-ups are graded at a later cycle (Add candidate → Make-up from an earlier cycle…), no fee, row marked “Make-up from [cycle]”.
  4. G9 (v0.946): tabs Candidates · Grading · Results · Rubric; Star v0.138–v0.144, v0.170, v0.248, v0.514 copied; Email result to the registration email, opened in the school’s own email; Mark as sent; a changed result shows “Not sent yet” again; admins edit the email texts; printed sheet; CSV admins only.
  5. v0.210: staff and admins print and email results; CSV, Choose subjects and the email texts are admin only; DOB and email never in a list.

R02. Email result — copy, open, Mark as sent, changed result, admin undo

Wed, Jun 9, 2027 at the desk (staff), and an admin on a tablet and a phone.

What it does

  • Staff press Email result on Mia Chen. The message is copied, and the school’s own email opens with only her address and the fixed subject. Staff paste (Ctrl+V), check it and press Send in the email — the dashboard can’t see that.
  • Back on the dashboard the row asks “Email opened — did you send it?”. Mark as sent records the date and who, with a 5-second Undo toast.
  • If a result changes after it was sent (a grade or an extra-points count corrected), the row goes back to “Not sent yet” with the earlier send noted, and Email result is offered again (tablet frame: an example on Ava Brooks; the cast’s numbers are unchanged elsewhere).
  • After the toast, only an admin can undo a Mark as sent, from ⋯, with a question first. Staff see the item grayed.

Rules applied (decided)

  • Star v0.140 / v0.141: the email opens in the school’s own email; “did you send it?” → Mark as sent → “Sent Jun 9 · Staff”; “Not sent yet” otherwise.
  • Star v0.248: the child’s name and result never go into a web address; the message goes through the clipboard. Star v0.514: same on every device (a plain mailto:, like Prospects), no fallback for now, admin undo later with a question.
  • Star v0.170: a changed result shows “Not sent yet” again, with the earlier send noted. G9: “to the email on the registration form” = the candidate’s “Email for results”.

Proposals on this screen (amber, still to confirm)

  • 12Email result in one click, as Star: (1) builds the message from the row and copies it as plain text; (2) reads the candidate’s “Email for results” for that one candidate at that moment; (3) opens mailto:<address>?subject=… with nothing else in the link.
  • 13Fixed subject for every family: “Your black belt testing result — U.S.T.C.” No name, Dan, score or result in it.
  • 14After the click the row reads “Message copied — paste it into the email (Ctrl+V).” (touch: “press and hold → Paste”) above “Email opened — did you send it? [Mark as sent]”. Kept in page memory only, until Mark as sent, another Email result or a reload.
  • 15Mark as sent records it at once: “Sent Jun 9 · Staff” (the desk account) or the person’s name for a personal login, and a toast “Marked as sent · Mia Chen” with Undo (5 s, pausing under the pointer, gone 9 s after the click at the latest). Logged.

R03. Result email texts — the block on Rubric and prices, and its edit dialog (admins)

Black Belt home → Rubric and prices, on Wed, Jun 9, 2027. The Waiver, Keeping and Default rubric blocks stay as built (drawn as one line each).

What it does

  • Admins edit the words of the result emails on Black Belt → Rubric and prices, in a new Result emails block. Each email kind has one text with blanks that fill in for each candidate.
  • The Edit icon opens Edit result email: the text, buttons that insert a blank, the checks, a live preview with a real candidate, and Reset to the starting text. Save, then a toast with Undo.
  • Staff see the block without Edit icons. The subject line is fixed and shown for reference.

Rules applied (decided)

  • G9 (v0.946): admins edit the email texts, like Star’s migration 0023. v0.210: admins only edit the Rubric and prices tab; staff view it.
  • Star v0.514 R11: the block, three texts, blanks in braces copied from Forms, Save blocked on a bad blank, live preview, Reset, saving like the other dialogs, changing a text never resets Sent; Q5: the current text is used when Email result is clicked.
  • v0.928 / v0.932: on phones a full-screen dialog; Discard is a plain link (as every Black Belt dialog).

Proposals on this screen (amber, still to confirm)

  • 21A Result emails block on Black Belt → Rubric and prices, between the Keeping block and the Default rubric card (always open, a row per email with the start of its text, an Edit icon per row for admins). Staff see it read-only under the tab’s “View only. Ask an admin to make changes.” — they send these emails, so they can read them.
  • 22Three texts, as Star: Passed · Make-up with a D (also used when the total is below the pass mark too; the extra sentence fills in by itself) · Make-up below the pass mark (after an admin chose the subjects).
  • 23No “Not finished” email (drawn striped, not proposed): Star has none, the email must say what to work on, and G2 says nothing goes to families by itself.
