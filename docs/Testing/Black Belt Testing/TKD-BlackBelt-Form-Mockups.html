Black Belt testing · Registration form — mockups

Jae H. Jeong's U.S. Taekwondo Center · Black Belt testing (Spring 2027 cycle) · public registration form · guidelines v0.220 · October 18, 2026 · Draft for review (amber items are proposals)

Design only — not a working app. Phone (390px, touch) first, then 320px where it’s tightest, tablet (820px, touch) and PC (1280px, in a browser). Names, emails, the link, the reference number and the signature are examples; subjects, credits, cut-offs, pass marks and fees are the real Fall 2026 values. Amber text = proposal, still to confirm.

The public page families open from a school’s Black Belt registration link (no login). It follows the answers to B5–B9 (TKD-BlackBelt-Form-Questions.html, TKD-BlackBelt-Payments-Questions.html) and reuses the approved Star form (TKD-StarTesting-Form-Mockups.html) wherever a Black Belt decision doesn’t change it. Sample cycle: Spring 2027 — Physical Sat, Apr 3 (Worcester + Horgan Arena), Technical Sat, May 1 and Curriculum Sat, Jun 5 (Shrewsbury); apply and payment 1 by Mar 27, payments 2 and 3 by Apr 24 and May 29, hand in by May 29. Fees: 1st Dan $870 (3 × $290), 2nd $990 (3 × $330), 3rd $1,500 (3 × $500), 4th $2,700 (3 × $900); 2 payments (Mar 27, May 29): 1st 2 × $435, 2nd 2 × $495, 3rd 2 × $750, 4th 2 × $1,350; Kukkiwon $100 / $110 / $140 / $170.

Already decided — drawn this way

  1. Fields and order: First / Last name (belt spelling), Date of birth (calendar), Testing for 1st–4th Dan, Kukkiwon certificate (optional, fee per Dan), How you’ll pay (every plan with real amounts and dates), total with “What [Dan] is tested on”, Email for results; school from the link; no sex, phone or parent fields (v0.211).
  2. Open form, counts at once, closes at the end of the application date; late families via the desk or an admin reopening (v0.212).
  3. Step 2: summary, versioned waiver + Read all, “I have read and agree”, signer’s name, drawn signature; a parent or guardian signs for under-18s on the first testing day (v0.213).
  4. Requirements behind one line, in a full-screen panel (dialog on a PC): parts → subjects → scoring, from the cycle’s rubric. Confirmation page with what to pay, testing days, Kukkiwon steps, requirements, BB-#### reference, print, register another (v0.919).
  5. Any number of payment plans, each with its own payments and amounts per Dan; Kukkiwon fee per Dan added to payment 1; due dates per plan (v0.217). Post-dated checks all count the day they’re handed in (v0.218).
  6. From Star’s form: two steps, Continue always active, amber field errors, one candidate per form + Registered on this visit, white header strip, 44px buttons / 16px fields on phones, Turnstile, can’t-register messages (v0.93–v0.106).

3. Requirements panel

Tapping “What 1st Dan is tested on ›” (v0.919, compact A as chosen in B8).

  • Phone: a full-screen panel over the form. ✕ or the phone’s back gesture returns to the form exactly as it was. Tablet and PC: a dialog over the form (proposed for the tablet too: the form stays visible around it); Esc, ✕ or a click outside closes it.
  • Level 1, the parts: one row per part with its testing day, place and number of subjects; then Other requirements (5 items; four are handed in by May 29, the Submissions due date; the assistant instructor course is not handed in), Extra points, and the pass rule: 275 points for children or 293 for adults (1st Dan; 2nd 261, 3rd 293, 4th 453), and no D in any subject.
  • Level 2, a part’s subjects: ← goes back to the parts. Proposed: each subject shows its credits on the right (“2 credits”), and the part ends with its maximum (“Physical: up to 80 points”).
  • Level 3, one subject’s scoring: tapping a subject opens it in place (+ / −). A physical event shows its cut-offs per age group (Push-up below); a graded subject shows the points for each grade (Power Break on the PC).
  • Everything comes from the cycle’s own rubric and testing days (the same data the grading uses), so it can’t disagree with grading. The numbers drawn are the real Fall 2026 ones from the old Sheet (subjects, credits, cut-offs, pass marks). Master Jeong’s part A answers may still change some of them; the extra-points numbers and the “Extra points count toward the total” line are to confirm with him.
  • Icons: ✕ ti-x, ← ti-arrow-left, › ti-chevron-right, + ti-plus, − ti-minus (chevron-right and minus are new to the Testing files).

5. Step 2 — check and send

Continue opens the check page with the waiver and signature (v0.213, Star v0.94).

  • Summary: cycle with its first testing day, candidate, school from the link, date of birth with the age on the first testing day, Dan and Kukkiwon, what to pay with the due dates, where results go.
  • The school’s waiver text with its version number and Read all; the “I have read and agree to the waiver.” tick; Name of the person signing; the drawn signature with Clear. Signed copies are kept a set number of years (insurer’s answer), then deleted (v0.213).
  • Under 18 on the first testing day: a parent or guardian signs; the hint says why (“A parent or guardian signs, because Alex is under 18 on Apr 3.”). Proposed: the signature label adds “(parent or guardian)”.
  • Adult: the candidate signs. Proposed: the name is filled in from the candidate’s name and can be changed.
  • Proposed: Read all opens the whole waiver in the same full-screen panel (dialog on a PC) as the requirements; ✕ returns to step 2.
  • Can’t sign on the phone? “Please sign at the front desk.” Staff then tick “Waiver signed on paper” (v0.213). Turnstile runs in the background (Star v0.105). Privacy note wording to confirm.
