Proposals on this screen (amber, not decided)

  1. 1Day switch: one choice per testing day (Sat, Apr 3 · Physical; Sat, May 1 · Technical; Sat, Jun 5 · Curriculum) plus Other requirements and Extra points — five in all. Each shows the date on top (“Today · Sat, Apr 3”, or “Any day”) and what is graded under it. Gray selected side, because it only changes the view (v0.89; keeps the blue count under rule 1.2). It fits in one row at 1280 and at 820; phones and 320 use a Day dropdown field instead.
  2. 2Age group, never the date of birth: gray text under the name (“Worcester · 10–12”), not a tag (rule 8). The group is worked out from all of the Dan’s brackets together (1st Dan: 8 and under · 9 · 10–12 · 13–17 · 18 and over), so one label fits every cut-off and the pass mark; ages on the first testing day (v0.926). Catch: a bracket edge one year apart (Target kick’s “9 and under”) makes a one-year group (“9”), which is the exact age.
  3. 3Measured cell: the number on top, the worked-out grade under it (gray; a D cell is amber); empty shows “·”. Selecting the cell opens a small editor under it: the number field with its unit, the way switch for a two-way subject, and the cut-off reached, worded by the rubric’s direction — “31 sec or more”, “8:30 or less”, and for Sprint 100 m (strictly less) “under 19 sec”. Enter saves and moves down to the next candidate in the same subject (one station measures one subject for everyone); Tab moves right; Esc closes.
  4. 4Number formats: count, reps and kicks are whole numbers; inches whole or .5; seconds with one decimal (11.8); minutes typed as m:ss (8:40). Long jump is typed in inches and the editor shows feet too (“70 in = 5 ft 10 in”). Allowed 0–999; anything else is refused with “Type a number from 0 to 999.” (a number outside every cut-off is D — decided).
  5. 5Two ways (Pull-up or hold): Pull-up is the starting way (the rubric’s first). The cell writes “Hold” before the number only when Hold was done. Switching the way keeps the number and works the grade out again from that way’s cut-offs. The switch is 44px tall on touch.
  6. 6Column headings: tilted names with credits (as Star v0.512), plus a gray line under each: the unit (in, count, sec, min, kicks; “reps / sec” for two ways) and “outdoor” for the outdoor subjects.
  7. 7An unfinished row (Star’s grid words): Total shows the running total of everything graded so far with “so far”; Result is gray “N of M left” (“16 of 25 left”), or “[subject] late (due May 29)” when hand-in work is late (late wins over “left”; three or more: “3 hand-ins late (due May 29)”); a D wins over both. “Left” counts only subjects that apply to the candidate (25 for 17 and under, 26 for 18 and over: One Step Sparring). The Results tab uses Star’s Results words instead: “Not finished · … not graded yet” and “Not finished · TKD project late (due May 29) · N more not graded”.
  8. 8Save wording: a measured number counts as a “grade” in the save messages (“Couldn’t save 1 grade (Rina Patel)…”, the Dan-switch ⚠, the leave question) and an extra-points count as a “count” — Star’s words, unchanged. Retry is 44px tall on phones.
  9. 9Upright tablet (820) — a deliberate change from Star v0.137 / v0.512: Total and Result move under the name, so every subject cell stays 44px or wider (the 44px tap rule) with no sideways scrolling, and names stand straight up so none run past the last column. Star instead squeezes its cells to 32px on the upright tablet (a named exception) — this avoids that exception. The sideways tablet (1180) keeps Star’s layout: full grid, Total and Result at the right end, like PC.
  10. 10Phone card for measured subjects: Star’s one-candidate card (◀ picker ▶). Each measured line: name, “credits · unit · the candidate’s bracket”, a 92px number field (number keypad), the worked-out grade next to it. A two-way subject shows a Pull-up | Hold switch (44px) in its line. The picker shows name and age group.
