  1. v0.921: a default rubric on Rubric and prices, editable by admins any time, used by every new cycle; each cycle’s own copy is editable until its registration first opens, then locked. Staff see the rubric read-only.

  9. v0.929: Undo in the “Registration opened” toast = never opened (the rubric stays editable).
  10. v0.930: a cycle’s first opening is admin-only; staff see Open registration grayed with “An admin opens it the first time (it locks the rubric).” v0.931: changed rows pale amber #FFF6DD. v0.932: plain Discard, Keep editing primary. v0.933: Add cycle always saves the cycle closed.

R1. Rubric and prices with the default rubric (admin)

The second tab of Black Belt home. The Waiver and Keeping cards stay as built; the Default rubric card is new, below them. Drawn: 1st Dan, with the cut-offs of Push-up and Pull-up or hold and the points of Egg test opened; and 4th Dan’s Technical part. Today: Mon, Feb 15, 2027.

What it does

  • Default rubric card (amber outline = new): a Dan switcher (1st–4th Dan; gray selected side, it only changes what is shown), the gray line “Used by every new cycle. A cycle’s copy can be changed until its registration first opens.”, and Edit 1st Dan (admins; the label follows the Dan shown).
  • To pass line, one pass mark per age bracket: “17 and under 275 points · 18 and over 293 points · no D in any subject”, the most points per bracket (339 for 17 and under, 361.5 for 18 and over) before extra points, and the make-up sentence (“A subject graded D is made up at the next testing, even if the total passes.”).
  • The four parts, each a card: name, worked-out maximum (Physical up to 80, Technical 44, Curriculum 135 / 157.5 for 18 and over, Other requirements 80), 1st Dan’s own grade points for that part (A+ … D; Other requirements has its own list), and one row per subject: credits, scoring, tags (Outdoor, Handed in, “Only 18 and over”, a note) and Up to (the subject’s A+ points).
  • Measured subjects have a Cut-offs toggle (text colour with a chevron); opened, it shows the subject’s own age brackets (Push-up: 8 and under / 9–12 / 13 and over, A+ … C). Pull-up or hold is one subject done two ways: Pull-up (reps; 12 and under / 13 and over) or Hold (seconds; all ages).
  • Own points are visible in the read view too: a dashed “Own points” tag on the row and a Points toggle that shows A+ … D, so nobody works out Egg test’s B as 5 × 2.5 = 12.5 (it is 11). 4th Dan (last PC frame): Target kick (30 sec) is measured and has own points, B = 9 (not 4 × 2.5 = 10).
  • Extra points (“added to the total, no limit”, amber “To confirm with Master Jeong”) and Handed in by Submissions due (Egg test, Meditation, Appreciation letter, TKD project).
  • “Last changed by Jeffrey B. · Feb 2, 2027” under the card.
  • Phones: subject rows stack (name and tags, then “2 credits · up to 8 · count, at least”); grade points become one line of text; in a cut-off table each bracket’s name sits on its own line above its five numbers, so nothing wraps or scrolls sideways at 320.

Rules applied

  • v0.921 — a default rubric on Rubric and prices, editable by admins any time, used by every new cycle; staff read-only. v0.922 — the four parts stay; a part’s maximum is worked out.
  • v0.925 — each Dan has its own grade points (all four start as today’s values). v0.926 — pass mark per bracket of the Dan’s list; each measured subject keeps its own brackets; One Step Sparring limited to 18 and over; ages on the cycle’s first testing day. v0.927 — Pull-up or hold done two ways.
  • Rule 1.2 — at most 5 blue elements: rail, location, ← Testing, the selected tab, Read all. The Dan switcher is a view switch, so its selected side is gray (v0.89, v0.247); Cut-offs is text colour.
  • v0.902 — prices stay on each cycle. v0.213 / OP-8 — Waiver and Keeping cards unchanged. 2.1 / 8 — 28px compact button with a mouse, 36px on touch; rows 36–40px (44px+ touch).

R4. A subject’s dialog: scoring, brackets, points, who does it

The Edit icon on a subject row. PC and tablet: a dialog over the editor; phone: full screen over the full-screen editor. Drawn: Push-up (1 min), measured, points worked out; One Step Sparring, graded, only 18 and over; Egg test, own points.

What it does

  • Name and Credits (also typed in the row on PC and tablet). Credits: a whole number from 1 to 20.
  • Scoring: Graded A+ to D (the grader picks a grade) or Measured: the unit (count, inches, seconds…), what a grade needs (at least / at most / under the number), and the subject’s own age brackets: from age, to age, and the cut-off for A+, A, B+, B and C. + Add age bracket, trash to remove one; + Add another way gives the subject a second way (R5).
  • Push-up today: 8 and under 20 / 18 / 16 / 14 / 12 · 9–12 36 / 30 / 25 / 20 / 15 · 13 and over 50 / 40 / 30 / 25 / 20. Below C is a D. Ages on the cycle’s first testing day.
  • Points: Worked out (Push-up: 3 credits × 1st Dan’s Physical grade points = A+ 12 · A 10.5 · B+ 9 · B 7.5 · C 6 · D 0, changing with the credits) or Own points (Egg test: each grade typed, A+ 20 … B 11 … D 0; the credits don’t change them). Switchable both ways.
  • Who does it: Everyone, or Only some brackets of the Dan’s pass-mark list (One Step Sparring: only 18 and over; replaces the old “adults only” tag). A bracket that doesn’t do a subject leaves it out of its most points.
  • Tags: Outdoor, Handed in, and a Note (e.g. 1/2 or 1 mile: “1/2 mile for 12 and under, 1 mile for 13 and over”).
  • Done puts the changes into the draft (the row turns tinted); Cancel leaves the row as it was. Unsaved changes in the dialog: Esc or clicking outside keeps it open with “Unsaved changes. Save or cancel first.” (v0.46); on phones the note has a Discard changes link (v0.56, v0.928; R10).
  • Phones: each age bracket is a small card (from / to, then the five cut-offs side by side), so the table never scrolls sideways, even at 320.

Rules applied

  • v0.922 — measured subjects (unit, direction, cut-offs), tags, note, credits. v0.924 — points worked out (credits × that Dan’s grade points for its part) or the subject’s own points, marked and switchable; the 19 Fall 2026 exceptions keep own points.
  • v0.926 — each measured subject keeps its own brackets; subjects can be limited to brackets (One Step Sparring = 18 and over). v0.927 — + Add another way.
  • v0.46 — centered dialog (12px corners) on PC and tablet, full screen with ← on phones. 2.4 — ti-check Done; ti-trash remove; ti-plus add.

R8. Errors on Save

Save 1st Dan pressed with mistakes in the draft. Nothing is stored; the editor stays open with the list at the top.

What it does

  • An amber box at the top of the editor, in the form-error style: “Can’t save 1st Dan yet. Fix these 7 things:” and one line per problem, naming the part and subject (or the bracket). Each line jumps to its field.
  • The same problems are marked where they are: amber borders with the reason under the field (pass mark 345, duplicate names, credits 2.5, Curriculum’s B 3.6, the Competitions step), and on a subject row whose problem is inside its dialog: “Check the cut-offs and age brackets” (opens Push-up, tablet frame).
  • Checks (the database checks every value too, v0.922): subject name empty or used twice in a part; credits a whole number 1–20; each part’s grade points going down from A+ to D; own points going down; cut-offs going the right way for at least / at most / under; age brackets (each way of a subject, and the pass-mark list) with no overlaps and no gaps; a pass mark not above its bracket’s most points; a subject limited to a bracket that exists; extra-point steps going up; at least one subject per part; names up to 60 characters.
  • Fields are also checked as you type (8 Forms), so most mistakes show before Save; Save catches the rest (e.g. a pass mark after a credit change).
  • Phones: the list sits at the top of the full-screen editor; tapping “Physical · Push-up (1 min)” opens Push-up with the bad cut-off and the gap marked.

Rules applied

  • v0.923 — Save checks the whole Dan and stores it in one step. v0.926 — no gaps or overlaps. 8 Forms — validate as you type; errors under the field. 3.5 — errors never auto-close.
  • Amber (warn) colours and ti-alert-triangle as the rulebook’s error style (§7, §2.4).

Spring 2027 on Mon, Mar 29, 2027 (registration first opened Mar 1, 2027); Fall 2027 on the same day as seen by staff (never opened); and Fall 2027 the moment an admin presses Open registration (Mon, Aug 2, 2027), with its own 18-and-over pass mark of 300 saved in R12.

What it does

  • Read-only for everyone, admins included: no Edit button. Gray line: “Locked since registration opened on Mar 1, 2027. Changes go into the default rubric for next season.”
  • A small lock icon before the line, and a “Go to the default rubric” link that opens Rubric and prices.
  • Open registration locks the copy at once, and the usual toast “Registration opened” offers Undo for 5 seconds. Undo puts the cycle back exactly as it was, including “never opened”, so its rubric is editable again (PC frame 2).
  • Once registration has really been open (the toast is gone), the rubric stays locked for good, even if registration is closed later: what families saw and printed, and what grading uses, never changes. A real mistake is fixed in the default rubric for next season (or by hand in the database, v0.921).
  • Staff see the same, under “View only. Ask an admin to make changes.” (phone 320 frame).
  • The first opening is admin-only: on a cycle that has never opened, staff see Open registration grayed in the cycle’s ⋯ menu with “An admin opens it the first time (it locks the rubric).” (staff frames, Fall 2027 on Mar 29). Once an admin has opened it, staff may close and reopen it as before (until the application date, v0.210). The database refuses a staff first opening too.

Rules applied

  • v0.921 — a cycle’s copy is locked once registration first opens. v0.929 — Undo in the “Registration opened” toast = never opened (stays editable); closing later doesn’t unlock. v0.930 — the first opening is admin-only (staff: grayed with the reason).
  • 1.5 — a disabled item says why (as the Cycles mockups’ “The application date has passed. Ask an admin to reopen.”).
  • 2.4 — ti-lock = locked state. 8 Toasts — 5 seconds with Undo.
