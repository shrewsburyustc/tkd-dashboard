Black Belt · Question B7: the waiver and signature, and how long the signed copy is kept

Step 2 of the form (left) is the same in every option: the summary, the school's waiver text (saved with a version number, so we always know which text was signed), an "I agree" tick, the signer's name and the drawn signature. If the candidate is under 18 on the first testing day, a parent or guardian signs. The question is how long the signed waiver is kept. Today's waiver releases the school from liability for injuries, so the signed copy is a legal record, unlike Color Belt's and Star's simple confirmation (deleted after 90 days). Amber = what differs. Not legal advice: the right number of years is a question for the school's insurer or lawyer.
