Stage 2 · Staff

Master Roster

One tab per Dan, each sorted from the lowest rank up — candidates with no stars first, then 1 star, then 2. Capacity is 100 per Dan. Click a payment pill to mark the fee received, or a school pill to move a candidate.

Stage 3 · Testing day

Grading Sheet

Choose a grade from A+ through D for each judged category. The two gold columns at the far right come off the school's own records — candidates never enter these. Enter the exact assistant-class count once, and the Assistant Instructor grade is calculated automatically. A D in any category means a make-up, even when the total passes.

Stage 4 · After the test

Final Scores

Passing is 245 of a possible 315 — the same mark for every Dan. A candidate passes only if they reach 245 and took no D. Anything short is listed as a make-up for the next testing.
