Star testing · Candidates tab — design questions

Jae H. Jeong's U.S. Taekwondo Center · Star testing · guidelines v0.90 · October 17, 2026 · One question at a time. Answered questions stay in this file with their answer.

Design only — not a working app. Names are examples. Option sketches for one decision at a time (PC and phone); full mockups come after the questions. Amber = proposed, not decided.

The Candidates tab is the first tab of an event page (v0.60): everyone registered for that star testing, from both schools (v0.58; the top-bar switcher filters Worcester / Shrewsbury). Already decided: the school comes from the registration link and staff can correct it (v0.80); duplicates come in flagged “Possible duplicate” (v0.79); each registration has a reference number staff can see (v0.85); make-ups are added by staff and don’t pay again (v0.78); list views never show date of birth, address, medical or billing details (rule 5).

Question 1 — How is the candidate list organized?

Answered 2026-10-17: Option B, as a full roster

Dan switch; each row also shows the date registered, how they’ll pay, whether they’ve paid and when (Jeffrey). Payment details in this staff list are a recorded exception to rule 5 — never on Grading or mat-side screens. Recorded as v0.90.

Seven example candidates across the three Dans and both schools. Rows show only name, school and progression here; what else a row shows is Question 2. The top-bar Worcester / Shrewsbury / Both switcher filters the list in every option (v0.58).

Option A — One list, a heading per DanRecommended

What it does

  • One list with a heading per Dan (“1st Dan · 4”). Inside each: lowest star first, then A–Z by last name; the school shows on every row (no school sub-groups — the switcher already filters by school).

Good

  • Everything on one page; easy to scan by Dan, which is how testing is run.
  • Works the same on every device.

Bad

  • A long page when many candidates register.

Recommendation: Option A

Testing runs Dan by Dan, and an event has usually a few dozen candidates, so one page with Dan headings is easy to scan and to print from. Choose D to keep the prototype’s school grouping inside each Dan. Choose C if finding one family quickly matters most (a list search can also be added to A later; it filters this event only, unlike the top-bar search of rule 3.2). Choose B to match the prototype’s Dan tabs.

Also answer: a device set to one school (e.g. the Worcester tablet) opens with the top bar on Worcester, so the list shows only Worcester candidates. Proposed: keep that (rule 3.3), with a line above the list “Showing Worcester candidates only · Show both schools” so nobody thinks candidates are missing.

Full roster — first draft (superseded by the next section)

From your answer to Question 1 (v0.90): the Dan switch, and for each candidate the date registered, how they’ll pay, whether they’ve paid and when. Example data, 1st Dan selected. How a payment is recorded is Question 4.

What it does

  • PC columns: Candidate · Testing for · Registered · Paying by · Payment (one tag: green Paid or gray Not paid) · Paid on · ⋯ — grouped Shrewsbury then Worcester, lowest star first, then A–Z.
  • Tablet drops the Registered column to fit (rule 1.3; still in the detail view). Phone shows stacked rows: name; progression and registered date; payment method and when paid; the tag.
  • Proposed: “Not paid” is gray, not amber — not having paid before testing day is normal, not a problem. One tag per row (rule 8): a “Possible duplicate” (v0.79) would show as an amber tag instead and win, as amber outranks green and gray.
  • Payment details here are the recorded rule 5 exception (v0.90): never on the Grading tab or mat-side screens.

Updated roster and Record payment

Confirmed 2026-10-17

Roster, Record payment dialog and the ⋯ payment items, as drawn. Recorded in v0.91.

From your three changes (v0.91): a Record payment button on unpaid rows, the registered date next to the name, and staff choosing how it was actually paid. Example: Alex Kim registered “Cash” but pays by check.

What it does

  • Unpaid rows show an outline Record payment button beside ⋯; paid rows show only ⋯. The name is followed by “Registered Oct 17” in gray.
  • Proposed dialog: Paid by Cash | Check | Card (starts on the form’s choice, with “They chose Cash on the form.”); Amount filled in from the event’s prices ($140 before the testing day, $150 on it; editable); Date paid = today; Check number (optional) appears only for Check. Save → the row turns Paid with the method actually used and the date, and a 5-second “Payment recorded · Undo” toast (rule 1.4).
  • Proposed: ⋯ also holds “Change how they’ll pay” for before payment, and “Undo payment” / “Edit payment” after (mistakes happen after the 5-second toast).
  • Card is for a card payment taken at the desk; families still choose only Cash or Check on the form until Square is added (v0.80).
