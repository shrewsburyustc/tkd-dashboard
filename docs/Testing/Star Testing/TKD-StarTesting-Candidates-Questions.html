Question 2 — What can staff do on the Candidates tab, and who sees date of birth and email?

The front desk uses a shared Staff account (v0.27). Elsewhere in Star testing: Rubrics and prices are admin only (v0.61); on events, staff can only open or close registration (v0.68). Every action is logged with who did it (rule 5; the desk shows as “Staff”). Whatever is chosen is enforced in the database. Removing and editing candidates, adding walk-ins and duplicates are designed in later questions; this only decides who.

Option A — Staff can do everything here

Good

  • The desk runs registrations and payments end to end; no waiting for an admin.

Bad

  • Anyone at the shared desk can remove a candidate or change a recorded payment; the log only says “Staff”.

Option B — Staff do the daily work; admins handle money corrections and removalsRecommended

Good

  • Recording payments, walk-ins, correcting a school or email, and sorting duplicates all happen at the desk.
  • Removing the extra copy of a “Possible duplicate” is allowed at the desk (logged, the real registration stays); removing a real candidate is not.
  • Changing money already recorded (after the 5-second Undo) and removing a candidate need an admin, so a mistake at the shared desk can’t quietly change the books.
  • Staff see date of birth and email in the detail view (needed to check a registration and fix a typo); never in the list (rule 5).

Bad

  • A desk mistake found later (wrong payment) waits for an admin.

Option C — Staff only record payments

Good

  • Tightest control; date of birth and email are seen only by admins.

Bad

  • The desk can’t fix a typo, add a walk-in on testing day or move a candidate to the right school without an admin.

Question 5 — How do staff add a candidate (walk-ins and make-ups)?

Two kinds of candidate don’t come in through the family form. Walk-ins: a family registers at the desk or on the testing day. Make-ups (v0.78): a candidate who got a D in some categories at an earlier testing, or scored below 245 with no D, retests only those categories and doesn’t pay again; staff add them. Staff and admins can both add (v0.92). Either way the new row lands in the roster like any other, with “Registered Oct 17” and no reference number. How a make-up looks in the roster is Question 10.
