Decision 8, rulebook v1.008 / v1.103 (approved M2). Plan §9 rows 9–16. The box’s look is already approved; this shows Star’s words in it, and the busy words to settle.

What it does

  • All seven boxes (9–15) are drawn by the shared ConfirmBox. The order stays: the button that changes nothing on the left (first focus), the action on the right; red when something is lost for good (9, 10, 13, 14), blue when it only reverses (11, 12, 15).
  • While it works the pressed button grays and says what it is doing; “Keep …” grays too; Esc, a click outside and Back wait until it is done. This closes the one safety gap: boxes 14 and 15 close today if Esc or Back is pressed while saving.
  • The two buttons stack full width when they don’t fit side by side (measured, so a stacked pair stays stacked while it works). The action keeps the width of its wider words, so nothing moves.
  • A failure shows the amber note under the buttons and the box stays open; pressing the action again tries again. Its words say the verb (Q2 B): “Couldn’t undo. …” (11, 12), “Couldn’t delete. …” (9, 10, 13), “Couldn’t undo Mark as sent. …” (15); box 14 keeps “Couldn’t save. …”.
  • Never the pinned button row (today box 9 picks it up on a screen under 700 px tall, plan §10).
  • Box 9 changes its words (Cancel → Keep category, Delete → Delete category); box 15 changes Cancel to Keep send record (Q1 A). All other words stay.

Decision 3, rulebook v1.001 / v1.104 (approved M3). Staff and admins see the same box. Example: Lily Brooks, 1st Dan, $140 (the price before the testing day), chose Cash on the form.

  • Q6 A (changes v0.110): staff can no longer type another amount; Mark paid records the fee, and an admin fixes a different amount with Edit payment.
  • After it saves: the row turns Paid, toast “Payment recorded” with Undo (unchanged).

DR2-P5. History becomes one line at the end of the summary (S6)

Decision 9b, rulebook v1.010 / v1.108 (approved M7). Example: Chris Kim, 15 changes.

What it does

  • The always-open History block (newest 5, then “Show all (15)”) becomes the line “History · 15 changes ›” at the end of the summary. A tap opens the list in place, newest first; the arrow points down while open.
  • Each entry: what changed, then the date only and who — “Oct 28 · Jeffrey Berg” (today “Oct 28, 2026, 2:07 PM · Jeffrey Berg”). The database keeps the exact time.
  • While it reads: “History” with a gray “Loading…”. If it can’t read: the amber bar “Couldn’t load the history. Check the connection, then try again.” with Retry (today a failed read shows “No changes recorded yet.”). Nothing recorded: “History · no changes yet”.
  • Hide private details on: payment changes are left out of the count and the list, and one gray line says “Payment history is hidden on this tablet.”; a date-of-birth / email change keeps who and when and reads “Email for results changed · details hidden on this tablet”.
  • Result-email rows (Q3 A): “Result email marked sent (Passed)” / “… (Make-up)”, and “Mark as sent undone” (today both say “Changed”).
  • Registered line while hidden (Q4 A): “Registered on the family form” / “Added at the desk” stays in the list and the count (it shows no money); only rows that change a payment or Paying by are left out.
