Question 1 — How does a private star testing fit in?

Answered 2026-10-18: Option B (changed from A)

A student who can’t make the testing day tests privately on their own day and time; they stay a candidate in that event and pay the private fee. Recorded in v0.147.

What we know: private star testing exists, and it has its own fee (1st Dan $160 · 2nd $170 · 3rd $180, v0.61). Nothing else is decided. The prototype only showed the fee. Grading (the 315-point rubric, the pass mark) would be the same as at a regular testing.

Answered 2026-10-18: their own separate grading (C), off the grid and paper sheet

Decided: a private student stays in the event (v0.147) and shows as “Testing privately” until graded (v0.149). Judges grade on the tablet or the paper sheet (v0.122). Decided: staff set the private day and time, and the fee becomes the private fee (v0.148). Example: Alex Kim, 1st Dan, private Tue, Dec 1, already paid $160 by check. Changing his private day or time is the same dialog in every option. The question is moving him back to the testing day after he has paid, when his fee would be $140 again.
