Jae H. Jeong's U.S. Taekwondo Center · Star testing, build phase 2 (Add event / Edit event dates) · guidelines v0.502 · October 19, 2026 · Approved 2026-10-19 (v0.503) — question B; after two design reviews and Jeffrey's screen-by-screen pass (revision 2, after the design review).

Design only — not a working app. Nothing here is built or deployed. Each screen is shown at PC (1280×800, mouse), tablet (820px, touch) and phone (390px, touch). Event names, dates, times and people are examples. “Today” is Sat, Oct 17, 2026, as in the Events mockups. Amber text, and parts with a dashed amber outline and a “Proposed: see N” label, are proposals to confirm (N = the number in “Proposals to confirm”); everything else is already decided.

Rulebook v0.502 builds the dashboard’s own calendar now, for the testing date and closing date in Add event and Edit event (approved Events screens 4–7 already draw them as “Sat, Nov 14, 2026”). Its look and behaviour are already approved — the date-of-birth calendar in the Form mockups, screen 4 — and are copied exactly. What wasn’t drawn is how it works for event dates: which days can be chosen, what happens to the closing date when the testing date moves, past events, where it sits on each device, and the keyboard.

Already decided — drawn this way below

  1. v0.502: the dashboard’s own calendar is built in Star phase 2 for Add / Edit event’s testing date and closing date; the family form (phase 4) reuses it.
  2. v0.107 (Form screen 4) — look and behaviour: white box, 1px border, 8px corners, light shadow; Month and Year dropdowns side by side at the top; Su–Sa; 36px days with a mouse, 44px with 16px numbers on the phone; chosen day blue-tinted; gray days can’t be chosen; gray hint line; no ‹ › arrows; opens on the chosen date’s month (March 2012, the 4th selected); closes on choosing a day; Esc or clicking outside closes without changing. Copied exactly (the CSS is the same).
  3. v0.85 / v0.92: date fields use the custom calendar; the closing date starts ticked at 7 days before the testing date and follows it until edited by hand; it must be on or before the testing date. Dates read “Sat, Nov 14, 2026”. Past events can be edited; moving a past event’s date into the future returns it to Upcoming.
  4. v0.500 (G4): with “Close automatically at the end of” unticked, the closing date field is hidden, so it has no calendar.
  5. CONTRACT-0006 (migration 0006): Add event — testing date today or later, closing date from today up to the testing date; Edit event — a changed date must be today or later, any closing date on or before the testing date.
  6. Rules: 2.5 (every tap area ≥ 44×44px on touch), 4 (2px school-blue focus ring; everything works by keyboard; nothing shown by color alone), 3.6 (Esc closes a dialog; unsaved changes ask first), 6 (detail view: centered dialog on PC / tablet, full screen on phones).

Question 2 — how the field shows the date

“Sat, Nov 14, 2026” — the approved format (Events screens 4 and 7; the Events list and event page use the same). Nothing new to decide; how screen readers read it is proposal 14.

Proposals to confirm

  1. Q1 (recommended B): event-date calendars keep Form screen 4’s look and behaviour, but drop the hint “Pick the month and year first, then tap the day.”; no ‹ › month buttons.
  2. Empty date: with no date chosen yet, the calendar opens on this month (Form screen 4 only drew a filled date).
  3. Testing date days: past days gray and unpickable; today has a thin gray outline; with a mouse the day under the pointer is light gray.
  4. Hint lines: testing date “Past dates can’t be chosen.”; closing date “● Testing date. The closing date can be from today up to Sat, Nov 14.”, the “● Testing date.” part only when the testing date’s month is on show.
  5. Empty fields and following: the testing date shows a gray “Choose…”; the closing date shows “Choose…” and can’t be used until a testing date is chosen; then it fills in 7 days before the testing date, never before today.
  6. The date field: a button, not a text box (nothing typed), with ti-chevron-down as Form screen 4’s field (Events screens 4 and 7 drew none). While its calendar is open it has a dark “open” border and ti-chevron-up, not the blue focus border; the same for an open Month / Year list.
  7. Placement: PC and tablet — a box floating under the field over the dialog, left edges lined up; above the field when there’s no room below. Phone — in the page, edge to edge, pushing the fields below down.
  8. Sizes: PC box 304px, days 36px tall; tablet box 336px, days 44px tall (each day ≥ 44×44); phone edge to edge, days 44px tall, 16px numbers, ≥ 45px wide at 320px (about 56px at 390px).
  9. Closing date limits: days after the testing date and before today are gray; the testing date has a dot and can be chosen; the Month list grays months after the testing date’s month (and before this month); the Year list stops at the testing date’s year.
  10. Past events (Edit): saved dates stay selected and can be kept; the closing date calendar grays every other day with the hint “● Testing date. The testing day has passed, so a new closing date can’t be chosen. Move the testing date first.”; unticking is allowed and ticking again brings back the closing date the event had when the dialog opened (G4); only when the event had no closing date when the dialog opened is the box gray and unusable, with the reason “Can’t be turned on after the testing day. Move the testing date first.” in place of the G4 hint; a closing date that has already passed never moves when the testing date is moved.
  11. When the testing date changes: a following closing date (exactly 7 days before the saved testing date; a date clamped to today at Add doesn’t count) follows on every change, later or earlier, in Add and Edit — 7 days before the new date, never before today; a hand-set closing date stays, unless it would end up after the new testing date, when it moves to the testing date itself with the gray note “Moved to Thu, Nov 5, so it isn’t after the testing date.”; a closing date that has already passed never moves (v0.85).
  12. Keyboard: Enter / Space / Alt+↓ on the field opens on the chosen day (or today); ← → day; ↑ ↓ week; Page Up / Down month (Shift: year); Home / End week start / end; Enter or Space picks and returns focus to the field; Tab goes Month → Year → days inside the calendar; focus can land on gray days, which are read “unavailable” (aria-disabled) and can’t be picked; 2px blue focus ring on the focused day.
  13. Esc order: an open Month / Year list, then the calendar (focus back on the field), then the dialog (rule 3.6).
  14. Screen-reader wording: the field “Testing date, Saturday, February 13, 2027” (“Closing date, …”); days “Saturday, November 14, 2026” plus “, today”, “, selected”, “, testing date” or “, unavailable”.
  15. Year list: this year and the next two (2026–2028), plus an older event’s own year in Edit event.
  16. Month list: months already over this year are gray; the list opens scrolled to the month on show.

Build notes (not design decisions)

  • On PC and tablet the calendar box is drawn outside the dialog’s scroll area (a portal / top layer), so the dialog never clips it or grows a scroll bar; it is placed against the field and flips above when there’s no room below.
  • The day grid uses real rows and cells (role="grid" / row / gridcell, or a table), one Tab stop with the arrow keys moving inside (“roving tabindex”); gray days get aria-disabled="true"; the chosen day aria-selected="true"; today aria-current="date".
  • The date field is a button with aria-haspopup="dialog" and aria-expanded; its name includes the full date.
  • One calendar component for the dashboard and the family form: it takes the allowed range, the month to open on and the hint text.

What it shows

  • Top row — first open, no date yet: Proposed (2): with no date yet it opens on this month (October 2026) — Form screen 4 only drew a filled date. (5): the field shows a gray “Choose…”, as Start time and Where in screen 4. (3): Oct 1–16 are gray and can’t be chosen (CONTRACT-0006, star_add_event: “today or later”); today (17) has a thin gray outline — a shape, not a color (rule 4) — and with a mouse the day under the pointer is light gray (PC: Oct 24).
  • Choosing a day fills the field and closes the calendar; Esc or clicking / tapping outside closes it without changing anything (approved with Form screen 4).
  • Choosing the testing date moves the closing date to 7 days before it (Feb 6) until the closing date is changed by hand (v0.85, v0.92); never before today (proposed, 5 — v0.85 / v0.92 don’t say it; it keeps the database from refusing it). (5): before a testing date is chosen, the closing date shows “Choose…” and can’t be opened. Save stays gray until name, date, start time and Where are filled (screen 4).
  • (6) The date field: a button, not a text box (nothing typed); it has the ti-chevron-down that Form screen 4’s date-of-birth field has (Events screens 4 and 7 drew none). While the calendar is open the field shows an “open” look — a dark border and ti-chevron-up, not the blue focus border — so the only blue ring on screen is the keyboard’s (C3).
  • (7) Where it opens: PC and tablet — a box floating under the field, over the rest of the dialog (the dialog doesn’t grow or jump; left edges lined up). Phone — in the page, edge to edge, pushing the fields below down (C4).
  • (8) Sizes: PC box 304px, days 36px tall; tablet (touch) box 336px, days 44px tall, so every day is at least 44×44px (rule 2.5); phone edge to edge, days 44px tall with 16px numbers and at least 45px wide even on a 320px phone.
  • (4) Hint line: “Past dates can’t be chosen.” (Q1: Form screen 4’s “Pick the month and year first, then tap the day.” is dropped for event dates.)

C2. The closing date calendar — Edit event (Add event works the same)

Admin clicks or taps the date under “Close automatically at the end of”. Top row: Fall 2026 Star Testing (testing date Sat, Nov 14, closing date Sat, Nov 7). Bottom row: past events opened from the Past list — Summer 2026 (Sat, Aug 15; closing date Sat, Aug 8) on PC and phone, Spring 2026 (Sat, May 16; no closing date) on the tablet.

What it shows

  • The closing date must be on or before the testing date (v0.85) and, when changed, not in the past (CONTRACT-0006, star_update_event / star_add_event). Proposed (9): so the calendar makes every day after the testing date (Nov 15–30) and every day before today gray and unpickable; the error “The closing date must be on or before the testing date.” stays as the database’s safety check.
  • (9) The limit is marked: the testing date (14) has a small dot under the number and can be chosen (closing at the end of the testing day). (4) The hint line: “● Testing date. The closing date can be from today up to Sat, Nov 14.” — the “● Testing date.” part only shows when the testing date’s month is on show (tablet, October: no dot, so no “● Testing date.”). Screen readers hear “Saturday, November 14, 2026, testing date”.
  • (9) Its Month list grays the months after the testing date’s month (and before this month); its Year list stops at the testing date’s year.
  • PC (1280×800): November 2026 with Nov 7 selected. (7) There isn’t room under the field on an 800px-tall screen, so the box opens above it.
  • Tablet: the admin switched the Month dropdown to October: Oct 1–16 gray, today outlined, Oct 18–31 can be chosen; Nov 7 is still the chosen date.
  • Phone: edge to edge under the closing date field; the screen scrolls so the whole calendar is in view.
  • (5) The closing date field can’t be used until a testing date is chosen (Add event: “Choose…”, dimmed).
  • When the box is unticked the date field is hidden (v0.500, G4), so there is no calendar.

Bottom row — a past event (proposal 10)

  • CONTRACT-0006 lets a past event keep its dates (only a changed date must be today or later). Proposed (10): its saved closing date (Aug 8) stays selected and can be kept; every other day is gray, because no closing date can be both after today and on or before Aug 15. The hint line says why: “The testing day has passed, so a new closing date can’t be chosen. Move the testing date first.” (PC and phone.)
  • (10) Unticking the box is allowed. Ticking it again brings back the closing date the event had when the dialog opened (as approved in G4, v0.500) — the database accepts an unchanged date — so for Summer 2026 the box stays usable and Aug 8 comes back.
  • (10) Only when the past event had no closing date when the dialog opened (tablet: Spring 2026, May 16) would ticking need a past date, which the database refuses. Then the box is gray and can’t be ticked, and the G4 hint is replaced by the reason: “Can’t be turned on after the testing day. Move the testing date first.”
  • Moving the testing date to today or later makes the event Upcoming again (v0.92). (10, 11) A closing date that has already passed never moves (v0.85: the date never reopens anything): Summer 2026 keeps Aug 8, so registration stays closed until someone opens it by hand.

C3. Keyboard use, and a testing date moved before the closing date

Edit event, Fall 2026 Star Testing (Nov 14, closing Nov 7). The Form mockups left “the calendar’s keyboard use” to be confirmed with its design (v0.107), so all of this is a proposal.

Top row — keyboard (proposals 12–14)

  • PC: Enter on the Testing date field opened the calendar on the chosen day (14); then ↑ ← ← moved focus to Thu, Nov 5. (12) The focused day has the 2px school-blue focus ring (rule 4) — the only blue ring on screen, since the open field shows the dark “open” look (6); the chosen day (14) keeps its blue tint until Enter picks another. A screen reader hears “Thursday, November 5, 2026”.
  • Tablet with a keyboard: (12) Tab from the days goes round to the Month dropdown (blue focus border, as every field); ↓ or Enter opens its list.
  • Phone with a screen reader (no ring drawn — the phone’s screen reader draws its own): (14) the chosen day is read “Saturday, November 14, 2026, selected”; today adds “today”, the testing date in the closing date calendar adds “testing date”, gray days add “unavailable”. Swiping moves day by day; double-tap picks.
  • (13) Esc goes back one step at a time: an open Month / Year list, then the calendar (focus back on the field), then the dialog’s own Esc (rule 3.6: closes, or asks first when there are unsaved changes, screen 7).
  • The ring only shows when the keyboard is used (clicking a day doesn’t draw it).

Bottom row — after Enter on Thu, Nov 5: the new testing date is before the saved closing date (proposal 11)

  • v0.92: “the closing date starts 7 days before the testing date and follows it until edited by hand”. It doesn’t say what Edit event does when the new testing date lands before the saved closing date (the database would refuse it).
  • (11) “Following” means the closing date is exactly 7 days before the saved testing date (Fall 2026: Nov 7). A following closing date follows on every testing date change, later or earlier, in Add and in Edit: 7 days before the new date, never before today — here Thu, Oct 29 (PC and phone). A date that Add event clamped to today (testing date less than 7 days away) doesn’t count as following.
  • (11) A closing date set by hand stays where it is, unless it would end up after the new testing date: then it moves to the testing date itself (tablet: Nov 12 → Thu, Nov 5) with a gray note under it: “Moved to Thu, Nov 5, so it isn’t after the testing date.”
  • (11) A closing date that has already passed never moves, following or not (v0.85: the date never reopens anything). Example: testing Oct 20, closing Oct 13, today Oct 17 — moving the testing date leaves Oct 13.
  • Nothing is saved until Save; Cancel puts both dates back.

C4. Phone layout, and the Month and Year dropdowns

How the calendar sits on a phone, and what the Month and Year dropdowns open. Form screen 4 drew the phone calendar in the page under the field (not a bottom sheet), so this follows it.

What it shows

  • Phone (third frame): Add event, Winter 2027, the closing date calendar open. Proposed (7, 8): the calendar sits in the page under its field and runs edge to edge (no side border or padding; the Month / Year row and the hint keep the 16px gutter), pushing the fields below down; the full-screen dialog scrolls as usual. That makes each day about 56px wide on a 390px phone and still about 45px on a 320px phone, 44px tall with 16px numbers (Form screen 4’s phone sizes). Feb 6 is chosen, Feb 13 (testing date) has the dot, Feb 14–28 are gray. A bottom sheet was not used because Form screen 4 didn’t draw one.
  • The Month and Year dropdowns are the dashboard’s own custom dropdowns (v0.51 look: white list, 32px items with a mouse, 44px on touch, current choice blue-tinted, scroll bar as the cue). Choosing a month or year redraws the days; the chosen date doesn’t change until a day is picked. An open list shows the same dark “open” look as an open date field (6).
  • PC — Year open: Proposed (15): the testing date’s Year list holds this year and the next two (2026–2028); in Edit event of an older event its own year is added. The closing date’s list stops at the testing date’s year (9).
  • Tablet and phone (fourth frame) — Month open, in October 2026: Proposed (16): months already over this year (January–September 2026) are gray and can’t be chosen; the list opens scrolled to the month on show, blue-tinted. In 2027 every month can be chosen.
