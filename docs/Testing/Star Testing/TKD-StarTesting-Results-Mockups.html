Star testing · Results tab — mockups for review

Jae H. Jeong’s U.S. Taekwondo Center · Star testing, build phase 5 (Results, printouts and email) · guidelines v0.146 · October 18, 2026 · Approved 2026-10-18 (v0.146) after the screen-by-screen pass; amber items stay open.

Design only — not a working app. Names, scores and emails are examples. Text in amber and parts with a dashed amber outline are proposed and still to confirm.

Decided — drawn this way below

  1. One Dan at a time, belt order, one tag with its reason (v0.138).
  2. No “final” step; results stay open; late grades update the result (v0.139).
  3. Result emails written by the dashboard, sent from the school’s own email for now; a sending service once there is a domain (v0.140).
  4. Email result, then “did you send it?” and Mark as sent (v0.141).
  5. A results sheet for the school with clear sections, big scores and boxed needs (v0.142), bright yellow Not finished, PASS, page filled (v0.143).
  6. CSV of everything, admins only (v0.144).
  7. Make-up highlighted in orange on the printed sheet (v0.145).

  • One message per result type, always with the total, the pass mark and, for a make-up, exactly what to work on. Wording still to confirm.
  • Still to confirm: opening the email without putting a minor’s name and result in a web address or browser history (rule 5).
  • Still to confirm: when a candidate has a D and is also below 245 (Noah), the row and email show both, and only the D subject is listed to work on.
  • Later, with a school domain, a sending service sends it straight from the dashboard (v0.140).
