Star testing · Phase 7 gap mockups

Jae H. Jeong's U.S. Taekwondo Center · Star testing, build phase 7 (Home “Today” card) · guidelines v0.514 · October 21, 2026 · Approved 2026-10-21 (v0.515) after a design review and Jeffrey's screen-by-screen pass; Q1 A, Q2 B.

Design only — not a working app. Nothing here is built or deployed. Small buttons are 36px on touch with a 44px tap area (§2.5). Screens at PC (1280px, mouse), the tablet held sideways (1180 × 820, touch) and upright (820px), phone (390px, touch), and 320px check frames. The rail is shortened to Home and Testing, as in the approved Home mockups. All names and counts are made up. Parts with a dashed amber outline and a “Proposed: see N” label are proposals (approved v0.515).

The approved Home mockups (v0.155) left these amber: the card’s look, the “Today” heading, how long the card shows, several private testings as separate cards, Grade opening the student’s own grading page, and the location-switcher counts. The build plan (TKD-StarTesting-Remaining-Build-Plan.md §3, row 7) adds Home while loading or offline. Since then the private grading page was built with no address (v0.512, v0.513), so how Home hands off to it matters, and Finance put its reminder on Home (v0.202). S1–S4 draw each one.

Already decided — drawn this way below

  1. Star on Home, only on the day (v0.154): a “Today” card for the testing (Open grading) or each private testing (Grade); nothing on other days.
  2. Home mockups approved (v0.155): TKD-StarTesting-Home-Mockups.html screens 1–4; this file reuses its look and data. Its amber items are the proposals below.
  3. Home itself (v0.10, v0.11, built): “today at a glance”; the welcome header; “Nothing on Home yet. Items appear here as sections are added.” while there is nothing else.
  4. Finance on Home (v0.202, v0.207, v0.811, built): the amber close-the-day lines under the header; no amounts; no link with Hide private details on.
  5. Private grading page (v0.512 Q1 A, v0.513): no address of its own; ← and Back go to Candidates; reload → Candidates; the three “page closed” banners; Hide private details changes nothing there.
  6. Hide private details (v0.506): one switch hides dates of birth, emails and payments on every screen.
  7. Database (contract 0006 §7): star_home_today() answers the events whose testing day is today (name, start time, where, counts per school of active candidates without a private day) and the private testings whose day is today (name, school, Dan, stars, time), by time; no payment, date of birth or email; the browser filters by school. It is a POST (it runs the signature purge). Nothing in this file needs a database change (only Q1’s B and C would).
  8. Dates used: Sat, Nov 14, 2026 (testing day); Tue, Dec 1, 2026 (private testings). Names and counts are made up and match the Home mockups.

Proposals (18) — approved v0.515

  1. S1 · The card’s look, as the approved drawing (v0.155’s first amber item): a ti-star in a blue-tint square, the title “Fall 2026 Star Testing · 10:00 AM”, one gray line “Shrewsbury school · 22 candidates testing today”, and a small outline button at the right (phone and 320px: under the text, lined up with it).   2. S1 · The “Today” heading (small gray capitals, as drawn) sits above the Star cards. For now only Star’s cards go under it; another section can put its own day items there later (its own design pass). Finance’s reminder lines stay where they are, straight under the welcome header and above “Today” — amber nudges first, as built (HomeReminders.jsx, v0.202).
  3. S1 · Open grading goes to the event’s Grading tab address, #testing/star/event/<id>/grading, and the tab opens exactly as when it is picked on the event page (its usual Dan, following the location switcher, v0.512). Browser Back returns to Home. Nothing is passed but the event id, which is already in addresses (v0.83).
  4. S1 · Count wording: “22 candidates testing today”, one: “1 candidate testing today”, none: “No candidates testing today” (the card and Open grading stay — the event is still today). The number is star_home_today’s count: active candidates without a private day, make-ups included, withdrawn never (contract §7) — Home never counts anything itself.
  5. S1 · How long the card shows (v0.154’s open item): all day, from midnight to midnight Massachusetts time (America/New_York, contract §1.6), whatever the grading state — the only option the database can tell without a change (Question Q1). The same for a private card.
  6. S1 · Several items on one day: every Star card in time order; at the same time the event comes first, then private testings by last name. Two events on one day: one card each. A private testing on the event’s day: its own card, as on any other day.
  7. S1 · When Home asks — copied from Finance’s Home reminders (useHomeReminders): on opening Home, every minute, when the browser tab becomes visible again, when the school is switched, and after unlocking; never behind the lock screen. So the card appears and goes away by itself at midnight (within a minute). star_home_today is a POST (it runs the 90-day signature purge, contract §5.7) — each call also does that check; it does nothing when nothing is due.
  8. S1 · “Nothing on Home yet” — proposed (new), not a copy: Finance alone shows the empty line at once, before its first answer. With Star’s card, the empty line waits until Star has answered at least once, then shows only when neither Finance nor Star has anything; it never shows next to a card. This is Question Q2’s option B (chosen, v0.515); with Q2 A it would show at once, as Finance does.
  9. S2 · Private cards as approved: title “Private star testing · 4:00 PM”, line “Alex Kim · 1st Dan · 1 star → 2 stars”, button Grade. One card per private testing (v0.155’s amber item), sorted by time. The name, Dan and stars are what star_home_today sends — never date of birth, email, fee or payment (§5).
  10. S2 · How Grade hands off (follows from v0.512 Q1 A and v0.513): Grade sets the address to the event, #testing/star/event/<id> (its Candidates tab — no candidate id), and hands the candidate id over in page memory only; the event page then opens the private grading page straight away, the same as Candidates ⋯ → Grade private testing, adding its one history step with the same address (v0.513). While it loads: the event page’s loading look, then the approved “Loading the private testing…”. Afterwards ← and Back go to the event’s Candidates tab, and one more Back returns Home.
  11. S2 · Changed since Home loaded (moved back to the testing day, withdrawn or deleted on another device): the event page opens on Candidates with the approved v0.513 banner (“Alex Kim was moved back to the testing day on another device. Grade them on the Grading tab.” and the two others). A reload on the private page goes to Candidates (v0.512). Home’s card goes on its next ask (within a minute).
  12. S3 · Location switcher on one school (v0.155 amber): the event card still shows (the event takes both schools), with that school’s count — “12 Worcester candidates testing today”, “1 Worcester candidate testing today”, none: “No Worcester candidates testing today” (card and Open grading stay). On Both: the total only, no split, as approved.
  13. S3 · “Shrewsbury school” (where the testing is) shows on every device — it says where, not whose.
  14. S3 · Private cards follow the switcher: only private students of that school (the candidate’s school, location_code); Both shows all. The filtering is done in the browser on star_home_today’s answer (contract §7: “the location switcher filters in the client”) — no new call when the school is switched, only the usual ask.
  15. S4 · Loading: no gray bars, no spinner (copied: Finance’s reminder has no loading look). The welcome header shows at once; the card appears when the answer arrives (usually well under a second). Proposed (new): until Star’s first answer, “Nothing on Home yet” isn’t shown (proposal 8) — so a slow or failed first ask never claims there’s nothing on the testing day (Question Q2 B, recommended).
  16. S4 · A failed ask keeps what was shown (copied from Finance: “a failed call keeps what was shown — a reminder is a nudge, not an error to report”). The next minute’s ask tries again. A lost connection and the answers stale and invalid all count as a failed ask; no_access locks the screen, as everywhere. What shows when the first ask fails: Question Q2.
  17. S4 · Hide private details on (v0.506): the cards are unchanged and both buttons work (grading works with the switch on, v0.512 H7) — they never had a date of birth, email or payment. Only the chip shows; Finance’s line loses its link, as built. Nothing new is hidden.
  18. S4 · Star build switch off (the live site until Star’s go-live): no star_home_today call and no card — Home is exactly today’s (build plan phase 7 check).
