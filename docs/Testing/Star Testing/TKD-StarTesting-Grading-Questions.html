Star testing · Grading tab — questions

Jae H. Jeong’s U.S. Taekwondo Center · Star testing, build phase 4 (Grading incl. paper sheet) · guidelines v0.121 · October 17, 2026

The event page’s Grading tab: where each candidate’s category grades, classes attended and assistant classes are entered on testing day. Known so far: each Dan’s rubric is 70 credits, pass mark 245 of 315, grades A+ 4.5 … C 2.0, D 0; a D anywhere means a make-up even with a passing total (v0.96); Assistant Instructor is never judged — it’s worked out from the number of assistant classes and the candidate’s age; classes attended is recorded; no sideways scrolling (rule 1.3); D shows amber, not red; payment details never show here (v0.108).

Question 2, changed — the grading screen as a grid, like the paper sheet

Confirmed 2026-10-17

“Yes”, with the subjects more clearly highlighted (drawn). Recorded in v0.124. Items marked proposed are still to confirm.

Your change: “like the roster with the names down the side and then all the subjects at the top so we can grade them on a large scale.” This replaces the By candidate | By category switch (v0.123). Drawn below on PC and tablet with no sideways scrolling (rule 1.3): one Dan at a time, every category in its own column. Subject headings are highlighted (Jeffrey): dark, medium-weight names on a gray band with a heavier line under it.

How it works (proposed)

  • Rows: the Dan’s candidates in roster order (v0.117). Columns: every judged category with its credits, then Total and Result (Passing, Make-up · D in …, N left, Not started). D cells are amber.
  • Entering a grade: click or tap a cell → a small row of grade buttons (A+ … D) opens under it; pick one and it moves to the next cell. On a PC you can also type the letter (Question 3).
  • Make-ups: categories they already passed show “kept” and can’t be graded (Sofia here).
  • Fits without scrolling: 1st Dan (10 categories) and 2nd Dan (12) fit at 1280px; on the 820px tablet the category names are tilted so 12 columns still fit. Phones are too narrow for the grid: they show one candidate at a time.
  • Assistant Instructor isn’t a column you grade; it’s worked out (Question 4) and included in Total.

Question 3 — How is a grade picked in the grid?

Answered 2026-10-17: Option C

Recorded in v0.125 (44px list items on touch). Items marked Proposed are still to confirm.

Grades are A+, A, B+, B, C+, C, D. Judges tap on tablets; staff copying a paper sheet are often at the desk PC with a keyboard (v0.122). Proposed for every option: after a grade is picked the next cell to the right is selected (a paper row is typed left to right); picking the same grade again clears it; Total and Result update at once.

Question 4 — Where are classes attended and assistant classes entered?

Answered 2026-10-17: Option A

Recorded in v0.126.

Each candidate has two counts: classes attended (recorded, doesn’t change the score) and assistant classes, which sets the Assistant Instructor grade (5 credits) from a table by age: 12 and under A+ at 15 or more … C at 10; 13 and up A+ at 20 or more … C at 10; under 10 is D. Staff usually count these from attendance before the testing day. The old prototype’s sheet had both columns.

Question 5 — Who can grade, and when do grades stop changing?

Answered 2026-10-17: Option B

Recorded in v0.127.

Grades decide stars and make-ups, so a change after results go out matters. Every grade change is logged with who and when (rule 5). Paper grades may be typed in days after testing (v0.122), so a lock tied only to the testing date could block that. Staff = the shared desk account and staff logins; judges grade with whatever account the tablet is signed in with.
