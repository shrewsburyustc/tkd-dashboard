Star testing · Phase 3 gap mockups

Jae H. Jeong's U.S. Taekwondo Center · Star testing, build phase 3 (Candidates, payments and private-testing setup) · guidelines v0.508 · October 19, 2026 · Approved 2026-10-19 (v0.508) after two design reviews and Jeffrey’s screen-by-screen pass.

Design only — not a working app. Nothing here is built or deployed. Each screen is shown at PC (1280px, mouse), tablet (820px, touch) and phone (390px, touch). All names, dates, emails and amounts are made up. “Today” is Sat, Oct 17, 2026 unless a screen says otherwise. Amber text, and parts with a dashed amber outline and a “Proposed: see N” label, were the proposals; all 40 were approved with the screens (v0.508), so everything here is now decided.

The approved Candidates mockups (13 screens, v0.121) and Private mockups (screens 1–4, v0.153) leave some decided things undrawn, and some states open. The build plan (TKD-StarTesting-Remaining-Build-Plan.md §3, phase 3) lists them: Record refund, the Dan-change confirmation, the switch that hides private details and every phase 3 screen with it on, New candidate at the desk, History and View signature, the make-up list’s empty and blocked rows, and loading / error states. Its second column lists the amber proposals of the approved files; those are drawn again here (G12–G15) so each can be confirmed on its own. G16 adds the payment corrections (Edit payment, Change how they’ll pay, Undo payment), and a last section checks the at-risk phone screens at 320px.

Already decided — drawn this way below

  1. Refunds (v0.166, v0.172 S7, v0.175): an overpaid amount is cleared with Record refund (amount, cash or check, date), saved as its own reversal row; admins only; never more than the overpaid amount; never dated before the candidate registered.
  2. Dan change after grading (v0.164, S8, OPEN-4–7): allowed after one confirmation naming the grades cleared, red “Change Dan and clear 4 grades”; on a paid row admins only; fee follows the new Dan, payment untouched, class counts kept, kept make-up grades cleared too.
  3. One switch: Hide private details (v0.506; builds on v0.161, v0.215, v0.249–v0.253): Finance’s live Hide payments switch is renamed “Hide private details” and its top-bar chip “Private details hidden”; it hides what v0.215 hides plus date of birth, email and every payment detail, on every screen, in every section; the whole dashboard keeps working; not enforced by the database; anyone signed in switches it; account-menu row after This device; the chip at the left of the top bar turns it off when tapped (phones: icon only); a new or reset device starts off. Exception: Color Belt’s “Not paid” grading tag (v0.250) — not on any Star screen.
  4. Answered questions (v0.507, all option A): New candidate is desk-only while details are hidden, and Edit details keeps date of birth and email in place as “Hidden on this tablet” (Q1); hidden values show the gray placeholder “Hidden on this tablet” (Q2); Star uses Color Belt’s 15-second quiet refresh, also for the Grading grid (Q3, v0.157, settles v0.165’s open item).
  5. Privacy (rule 5, v0.160, v0.169): DOB and email only in the detail view (and the approved compare dialog), never in a list; History shows DOB / email changes before → after in full on desk devices; the signature only “on file”.
  6. New candidate (v0.111, S3, OPEN-19, OPEN-20, v0.175): desk rows may have no DOB; email required; no “Paid now” tick; DOB uses the dashboard calendar (v0.107 look, built v0.502/v0.503).
  7. Make-ups (v0.223, OPEN-11, §5.8): only from earlier dashboard testings; choice_needed can’t be added until an admin chooses subjects; “Already added”.
  8. States (rules 3.4, 3.5, 8; v0.81; v0.500 G1–G3): skeleton within 1 second; amber banners with Retry that never auto-close; dialog notes under the buttons; Color Belt’s live wording.
  9. Approved layouts reused as they are: roster, cards, Dan switch, Record payment, ⋯ menus, compare, detail summary, Edit details, Withdraw / Delete (Candidates v0.121); private row, Set private testing, overpaid line (Private v0.153).

P3-G1. Record refund (admin)

Alex Kim paid the $160 private fee, then moved back to the testing day (v0.152): his row reads “Paid $160 · ⚠ $20 overpaid”. The school hands back $20. Admin Dana Ross, Sat, Oct 24.

What it shows

  • Decided: an overpaid amount is cleared by recording a refund (v0.166): amount, cash or check, date. It is saved as its own refund row in the payments (payments are never edited or deleted). Only admins record a refund, and the database refuses a refund larger than the overpaid amount (S7, contract §6.4 refund_too_large). The date can’t be before the day he registered (v0.175) or after today.
  • Proposed (see 1): Record refund sits in the admin’s ⋯ only on a row that shows “overpaid”, after Undo payment, with a new icon ti-receipt-refund. Staff never see it.
  • Proposed (see 2): the dialog has Refunded by Cash | Check (no Card), Amount filled in with the overpaid amount, Date refunded = today in the dashboard calendar with future days and days before the registration date gray. Typing more than the cap shows “Can’t be more than the $20 overpaid.” under the field. If nothing is overpaid any more when Save arrives (someone else refunded first), the note under the buttons reads “Nothing is overpaid any more. Someone else changed this just now. Close this to see the latest.” (third row, phone).
  • Proposed (see 3): after Save the row reads “Paid $140” (the Paid tag, no amber), Payments recorded drops by $20, the toast says “Refund recorded · Undo”. The detail’s Payment line lists both: “$160 check · paid Oct 17 · refunded $20 cash Oct 24”.
  • Proposed (see 4): while the refund stands the database refuses Edit payment and Undo payment on the original payment (“undo the refund first”, contract §6.4). The admin’s ⋯ shows Undo refund, and Edit payment / Undo payment grayed with “Undo the refund first.” (bottom row). Record refund is gone (nothing is overpaid).
  • While Hide private details is on, none of this is reachable (G4).
