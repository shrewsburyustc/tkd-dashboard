My recommendation was: A. Emails go out after the event, case by case, sometimes weeks later; the point of editing the texts is to fix them for the emails still to send. A copy per event would trap a typo in every event created before the fix, or need a second editor. Rubrics and prices are different: they decide scores, so they must not change under an event; wording doesn’t change anyone’s result (and never resets “Sent”, proposal 52).
