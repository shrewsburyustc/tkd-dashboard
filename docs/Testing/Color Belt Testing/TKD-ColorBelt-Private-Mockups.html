Color Belt testing · Private testing — phase 4 mockups

Jae H. Jeong's U.S. Taekwondo Center · October 18, 2026 · for screen-by-screen approval · built on the live phase 3 event page (Grading mockups G1–G8, v0.190, live v0.196) and Star's approved private testing (Star v0.148–v0.154). Example: November Children Testing, Fri, Nov 20, 2026, Worcester school. Ava Chen (Group A, Yellow → Orange) can't come on Nov 20; on Tue, Nov 10 the desk sets her private testing for Tue, Dec 1 at 4:00 PM (P2 → P3), and the row then looks like P1. There are two ways in: ⋯ → Set private testing for a registered student who changes plans (P2 → P3), or the “Tests privately” switch in Add student when the desk adds her already private (P3b). Names are examples, not real students.

Solid amber outline = new on this screen, already decided (Q1–Q6)Dashed amber outline, amber text = proposed, to confirm

Decided

Jeffrey's answers to the six phase 4 questions. Everything drawn without a dashed outline follows these, the live app or an approved rulebook row.

Q1What a private testing is. A registered student who can't make the event's testing day and tests privately on their own day and time. They stay in the event (like Star v0.148).
Q2Star's private design is the base, adapted. ⋯ → Set private testing on the Students tab. The price becomes the rank's private price from Classes and ranks (Group A Yellow: early $65, testing day $75, private $85; Group B Blue: $70 / $80 / $95); Mark paid charges it. A purple Private tag line under the name: “Tue, Dec 1 · 4:00 PM · $85”. Private students are left off the testing-day Grading tab and the printed grading sheet; they have their own private grading page with Pass / Not passed / No show and the note button. The phase 3 lock (v0.186: before the day grayed, on the day staff and admins grade, after the day staff read-only, admins can change) counts from the private day. ⋯ items: Change private day or time, Back to the testing day (<date>), Grade private testing.
Q3Who. All staff (including the shared front desk “Staff”) can set a private testing. The price is never typed.
Q4Already paid, then private at a higher price. The payment stays, the row stays Paid, and an amber line “Paid $65 · $20 more to collect” shows under the name. Mark paid then records just the difference and the line goes. Paid the private price, then Back to the testing day: the payment stays, nothing owed.
Q5Where staff find them. A “Private testings” list on the Events tab, above Upcoming / Past: each upcoming private testing with date · time · student · class · event and an Open button to the student's private grading page. Graded ones drop off.
Q6Which days. Any day from today on, before or after the testing day; past days are refused. The Set private testing dialog: Day (browser date field, with the weekday written out under it, like Add event v0.157) + Time (5-minute steps) + the price change shown before saving (“$65 → $85 private price”).
Q7Add student can add someone already private. Add student gets a “Tests privately” switch. Off by default (the dialog is exactly the live Add student dialog). On: Day (date field + weekday written out underneath) + Time (5-minute steps) + the price line “$65 → $85 private price” appear in a section under Paying by; one Save adds the student already set as private (the roster row then looks like P1). ⋯ → Set private testing stays for students who change plans later. Families still can’t choose private on the online form (unchanged).

P3. Set private testing dialog

What it shows

  • Tue, Nov 10, before saving (opened from P2). Title “Set private testing”, subtitle with the student, class and belts.
  • Day: the browser’s date field; once a valid day is in, the weekday is written out under it (“Tuesday, December 1, 2026”) so a wrong day is easy to spot (like Add event, v0.157). Any day from today on, before or after the testing day.
  • Time: a list in 5-minute steps (like the event class times, v0.157).
  • The price change, before saving: “$65 → $85 private price”, with where it comes from and what Mark paid will charge. No price field (Q3).
  • Save adds the private line (P1). Change private day or time (P5) opens the same dialog with the current day and time filled in.
  • A past day: the field turns amber with “The private day can’t be in the past.” and Save does nothing until it is fixed (tablet frame). The database refuses a past day too.
  • Phone: full screen, back arrow, Save in a footer pinned to the bottom edge (v0.194). Unsaved changes: the app’s usual “Unsaved changes. Save or cancel first.” (v0.56).

Follows

  • Q6 (day, time, price shown first), Q2 (private price), Q3 (never typed); Star v0.149 (day, time, fee change); v0.157 (weekday line, 5-minute steps); v0.194 (phone Save at the bottom).

Proposed — to confirm

  • No toast after Save (the private line on the row is the confirmation; Back to the testing day undoes it). In Change private day or time: the title “Change private day or time” and no price line (the price doesn’t change).
  • The short line under the price (“Ava stays in this event. On her day she is graded on her own page, not on the Grading tab.”). Already paid at the early price: the price line would add “Paid $65 · $20 more to collect after saving” (P4).

P3b. Add student with “Tests privately”

What it shows

  • The live Add student dialog (R4: first and last name, Class, Current belt with the “Testing for …” line, the 3-stripes tick, Paying by, the walk-in hint) with one new section under Paying by: Tests privately, with the gray hint “Can’t make the testing day, Fri, Nov 20.”
  • Off (the default, tablet frame): the dialog is exactly today’s Add student dialog plus the switch line.
  • On (PC and phone frames, filled for Ava Chen, Group A, Yellow, 3 stripes ticked, Cash): Day (date field, weekday written out under it), Time (5-minute steps) and the price line “$65 → $85 private price” appear inside the section. One Save adds Ava already set as private, so her row looks like P1. The same rules as P3: no past days, the price is never typed.
  • ⋯ → Set private testing (P2 → P3) stays for students who change plans later. Families still can’t choose private on the online form.
  • Phone: full screen, back arrow, Save in a footer pinned to the bottom edge (v0.194); the section scrolls with the form.

Follows

  • Q7 (the switch, its fields, one Save), Q2 (private price, Mark paid charges it), Q6 (day, time, price shown first, past days refused), Q3 (all staff); phase 2 Add student (R4); v0.157 (weekday line, 5-minute steps); v0.194 (phone Save at the bottom).

Proposed — to confirm

  • The control: the app has no switch component, so “Tests privately” is drawn as a tick box styled like the live “Has 3 stripes” tick (dashed outline on the box). A real on/off switch would be a new component.
  • The Day / Time / price fields are indented under the tick, so they read as belonging to it; a past day shows the P3 message under Day.

P4. Already paid, then private at a higher price

What it shows

  • Another case of the same story: Ava had already paid $65 (early price) on Nov 2 when the desk set her private on Nov 10. Viewed Thu, Nov 12. The payment stays as recorded and the row stays Paid. An amber line under the name says what is left: “Paid $65 · $20 more to collect” — on every device, and on the tablet across the full row width so it never wraps mid-phrase.
  • Mark paid records just the difference ($20, by her Paying by method) and the amber line goes. The usual toast: “Ava Chen marked paid · $20 cash · Undo” (rule 1.4); Undo brings the line back.
  • The cards count her as Paid in both states; Payments recorded goes from $520 to $540.
  • The other way (Q4): paid the private price ($85), then Back to the testing day — the payment stays, nothing is owed, no amber line (P5, tablet).
  • The 320px phone (narrowest supported) shows the private line and the amber line fit.

Follows

  • Q4; live Mark paid and its toast (plan answer 6, markedPaidToast); Star v0.152 (amber payment line under the name).

Proposed — to confirm

  • Mark paid next to a Paid tag while the amber line shows (dashed; the alternative is Mark paid only in ⋯). The warning icon on the amber line (Star uses ⚠ there too).
  • A small amber “1 owes more” under the Paid card while anyone has an amber line (dashed).
