Jae H. Jeong's U.S. Taekwondo Center · October 19, 2026 · for screen-by-screen approval · rewritten after the owner tried the built version and redirected phase 4 (rulebook v0.401): private testing is its own tab, not part of any testing event. Built on the live Color belt testing page and event page (phase 2 rosters, phase 3 Grading G1–G8). Examples: Ava Chen (Group A, Yellow → Orange) moved out of November Children Testing to Tue, Dec 1, 4:00 PM; Liam Park (Group B, Blue → High Blue) and Noah Kim (Group A, Orange → Green) added straight on the tab; Emma Ross cancelled. Decided

The owner's redirect of 2026-10-19 (v0.401) and the three new private questions. Earlier answers that still hold are listed after them; the ones the redirect replaced are marked withdrawn.

v0.401Private testing is not connected to any testing event. Testing gets a third tab, “Private testing”, at the far right of the tab bar (after Events and Classes and ranks, pushed right). Staff add a private testing when a family asks. It has its own day and time, the rank’s private price from Classes and ranks (Group A Yellow → Orange $85, Group B Blue → High Blue $95), its own payment and its own grading. Event rosters and Grading tabs hold only testing-day students. Families can’t choose private online.
Q1Move to private testing in the event roster’s ⋯ (all staff): pick day and time; the student leaves the event (listed under the event’s Removed section as “Moved to private testing, Tue, Dec 1”) and appears on the Private testing tab with name, class and belt filled in. Their payment moves with them (paid $65 early → private $85 → amber “Paid $65 · $20 more to collect”; Mark paid then records $20).
Q2The Private testing tab is a roster like an event’s Students tab: Upcoming / Past switch, summary cards (Private testings · Paid · Not paid · Payments recorded), header button “+ Add private testing” (all staff), one row per private testing: day · time, student (+ class), Testing for (belts) · price, Paying by, Payment tag, Paid on, Mark paid on the row, ⋯ = Edit details · Change day or time · Grade · Cancel private testing (staff) · Delete permanently (admin). Grade opens the grading page (Pass / Not passed / No show + note, Print a one-student sheet); the lock counts from the private day: before → “Grading opens on the private testing day, <day>.”; on the day staff and admins grade; after → staff read-only, admins can change.
Q3Cancel private testing (staff): it stays listed under Past with a gray tag “Cancelled <date>”; any payment stays (not refundable); admins can still Delete permanently.
Still validThe Add private testing dialog: name, class buttons, current-belt buttons, 3-stripes tick, Paying by, Day (date field + weekday written out), Time (6 AM – 10 PM, 5-minute steps), the price line “$85 private price”; any day from today on (past days refused). Only a result counts as graded. A belt correction to a pricier rank after paying → “Price $85 → $95 · $10 more to collect”. Change how they’ll pay while money is owed. One-click Mark paid in full. All staff can do all of this (old Q3); the price is never typed. “they / their” wording.
WithdrawnOld Q1 (a private student stays in the event); the event-roster parts of old Q2 (⋯ → Set private testing on the event’s Students tab, the purple Private line under the name, left off the event’s Grading tab, ⋯ Change private day or time / Back to the testing day / Grade private testing); old Q4’s “Back to the testing day” case; old Q5 (a Private testings list on the Events tab — replaced by the tab); old Q7 (Add student’s “Tests privately” switch). Old Q6 lives on in the dialog above.
