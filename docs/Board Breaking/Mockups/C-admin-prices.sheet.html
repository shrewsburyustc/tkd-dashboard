Board breaking · Chart and prices (admins only) · Prices and Age groups — mockup

One more tab on the Board breaking page, drawn with the app's own stylesheets and icons. Saving copies Color belt's Edit prices and Star's Edit fees: a pencil opens a dialog, Save closes it, a toast offers Undo. Numbers are fake. Amber outline = what is new.

1The top of “Chart and prices” (admins only). Two cards in the look of Star testing’s Fees block: a title, a pencil, rows. 1 · Prices (all events) and 2 · Age groups. The board chart comes under them (next picture). Staff never see this tab.

2Edit prices (the pencil on the Prices card): a dialog like Star’s “Edit fees”, in dollars and cents, Cancel / Save, with the worked example read-only. On the phone it is a full-screen page with one full-width Save.

3After Save: the dialog closes and the toast says “Prices saved” with Undo for 5 seconds, as Color belt’s Edit prices does. The card shows the new numbers at once. A failed save keeps the dialog open with the app’s amber “not saved” note (rule 3.5).

4Edit age groups (the pencil on the Age groups card): one number box. “Teen / Adult” follows by itself (the number plus one).
