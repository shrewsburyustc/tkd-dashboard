Board breaking · Family sign-up page: after Register, and closed (mockup)

What a parent sees after tapping Register (card, card with a failed payment link, cash or check) and when registration is closed. Same look as the Camps "You're signed up" and "Sign-up is closed" pages. Amber outline = new on this page. Names and amounts are made up.

1Done, card: "Ava R. is registered", what they signed up for, the total and the primary Pay $13.00 with Square button. The amount is the database's amount, not the browser's.

2Done, card, link failed: Square could not make the link, but the registration is saved. An amber note, a quiet Try again button and "Or pay at the front desk."

3Done, cash or check: "Registered. Please pay the school by cash or check before the event day." with the total and a quiet Register another student button.

4Closed: for an event past its date or switched off. The same look as Camps' "Sign-up is closed". No form.
